Old Danish QuakeWorld clan.

## History

Danish QuakeWorld clan founded in 1996 by Zombie and Beast. The clan was fairly active until Quake 2 was released, which led Zombie to leave QuakeWorld in order to focus on playing Quake 2. Clan Armageddon was later revived again under the lead of KickYourAss. However, the revival didn't last long and the members eventually left QuakeWorld when Quake 3 was released in 1999.
In Quake 2 the clan won NQL several times.
