## History

Psycose Team (psY) is a Brazilian multi-gaming org started in 2025 after the disbandment of the revival of Savage Hunters, founded by bpx, Pegaso (pg) and X1bot. Though X1bot stepped back shortly after foundation, the legacy of the team continued by the other members.

The team is mainly focused on QuakeWorld and generally sponsored by the BPX Foundation.
The org previously played SUPERVIVE and current members are still engaged in ARC Raiders and Overwatch.
