## History

'''Followers of the Bananameister''' was a famous Finnish clan formed in mid 1997 by players Image:flag_fi.gif Vekotin and Image:flag_fi.gif Verbatim. The clan was active in both Deathmatch and Team Fortress. Image:flag_fi.gif Vekotin later decided to rename both sections of the clan to "Friends of the Banana". When Image:flag_fi.gif Anza later stepped in as the team leader he decided to rename the clan back to its original name: '''Followers of the Bananameister'''.

The clan disbanded in early 2000 when Team Fortress had pretty much died out and Image:flag_fi.gif Anza decided to join legendary Finnish clan Image:flag_fi.gif Fraggers United.

Most famous players for this clan would be Image:flag_fi.gif Jester, Image:flag_fi.gif Omny, Image:flag_fi.gif Isotee, Image:flag_fi.gif Carnage and Image:flag_fi.gif Karhu.
