The ARD (ARmageDdon) clan was formed in a computer club (Internet cafe) in the Moscow Institute MIREA.<br> The computers allowed Quake to maintain 72 fps in 320x200 mode, which was an achievement at the time :)<br> Initial lineup:<br> Spazm (the author of these lines)<br> Warrior (top player before Slam came)<br> Slam (our Buryat friend)<br> Bloodliker<br> Tlya<br> Kreker<br> Dragon<br> Cracker<br> <br> One day we were hanging out in the club as usual, an unknown guy of Chinese appearance came to the club and knocked out our undisputed champion Warrior almost in the minus.<br> When asked how he learned to play like that, Slam answered that he plays on the Internet, after which many of us became concerned about entering the Internet arena. Through 14400 modems, with a ping of 180-190, they began to master quake.ru :)<br> <br> In 1998, the clan moved under the wing of the Arena club in Yuzhnoye Butovo, where by that time a normal Internet had already appeared and the opportunity to play in Europe with pings less than 50.<br> <br> The composition of the clan changed dramatically... most of the old players created a new clan, Armageddon DeepTown.<br> (c) Spazm
