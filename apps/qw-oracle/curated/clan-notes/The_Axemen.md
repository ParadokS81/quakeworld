Ambitious, but unskilled Norwegian Quake-players. === History === The Axemen is an old school Norwegian clan from the early days. They played until 2004 before a longer break, and came back again in 2008 to participate in EQL9 with a mix of core members and others. Today they are one of the most active clans in the community, and can often be seen lost in the halls of dm2.
