## History

Founded in 1996, Eradication on Arrival were a more relaxed clan and didn't really stick to any regimented training schedules like some of the bigger clans.

The clans biggest moment was winning UKCL Division 2, but then were not allowed to promote to Division 1 by admin Bigfoot (damn you Bigfoot).

Members were primarily from UK and Sweden.

## Achievements

* 1st MCW Season 6 Division 3
* 1st UKCL Division 2
