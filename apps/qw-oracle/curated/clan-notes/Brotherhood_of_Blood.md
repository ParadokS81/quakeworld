## History

Aerotus (at this point called Phantom) used to run a Duke Nukem 3D ladder tournament. Many of those players got together and formed or joined this Quakeworld clan.

Later more or less the same players became the Dark Deviants and then SaS before finally merging with Fallen Angels to become the famous UK clan Denial.
