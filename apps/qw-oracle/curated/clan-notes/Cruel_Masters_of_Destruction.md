Initially the clan was organized by an forgotten player and was called DMQC (Dynamo Moscow Quake Clan). Most players used didial-up connection to play FFA. Several people joined the clan, among them were Mutabor, gLAd, White-Hot. Some time later, gLAd moved to Radiation Decay, other old players stopped playing and White-Hot became the leader. He renamed the clan to CMD (Cruel Masters of Destruction) and recruited a new team. Later, White-Hot and Mutabor also left for RDY and left the clan to young fighters.
