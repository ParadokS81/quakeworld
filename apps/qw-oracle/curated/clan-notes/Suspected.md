## History

Suspected was founded by Mille and a few other beginners in the year 2002. They participated for the first time in NQR Season 2. After a while the clan was closed, because of inactivity and players taking a break from QW. The clan was reborn a few years later, with some old players and some new players who had improved a lot, but then again the clan was closed and will probably stay that way.
