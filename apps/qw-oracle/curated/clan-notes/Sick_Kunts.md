Legendary Quake clan from Europe. Formed by five gamers - bjarkeSTAR, gosciu, kane, rokky and snapcase.

## History

Started out as a LHP: Y1–4 clan. Today Sick Kunts, SK, SK-Gaming, is the worlds leading single-gaming clan with singular squad in one game. Their discord server is also the biggest e-Sport portal in the world.
