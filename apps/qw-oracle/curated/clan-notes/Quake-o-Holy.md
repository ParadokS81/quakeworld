## History


Beginning in late 2005, former Quake-o-Holics member Lornelin recruited three pals from his clan Egda to form a new QW clan with Christian members only. Quake-o-Holy ([http://wiki.qwdrama.com/Qoh Qoh]) played the first season in EQL and had some successes before falling victim to inactivity. The clan disbanded shortly after [http://wiki.qwdrama.com/EQL_Season_1 EQL1] had finished, with ui joining a2k2 and Stroller going to [http://wiki.qwdrama.com/SuddenDeath_Project SuddenDeath Project]. Lornelin went inactive, and xazqe went back to idling in [http://wiki.qwdrama.com/Bad_Luck_Troopers BLT].
