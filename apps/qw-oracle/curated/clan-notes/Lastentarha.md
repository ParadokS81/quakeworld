Lastentarha means Kindergarten in Finnish.

## History

The team that can be seen as the spinoff of the great KOFF team. Quite some time after Gamer, Jukka and Eh quit some of the remaining KOFF players decided they wanted to play again. Scenic and Nicam (Pkk on paper, but he never played much 4on4) started this team, even playing with the tag KOFF.

## Achievements

* 2010 - 12th place: EQL Season 12 Division 1
* 2011 - 2nd place: EQL Season 13 Division 2
