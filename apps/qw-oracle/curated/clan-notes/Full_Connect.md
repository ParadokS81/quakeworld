## History



QuakeWorld is much more than a game. If you like this game, certainly you've already heard about the histories, glories and victories of a great clan.
Histories about friendship and cooperative play that unifies players of the whole world.

Based on strong bond of friendship, trust and loyalty, people from every place of Brazil helped to build "Full Connect Quake Clan".
But the most important thing is that Full Connect is like a family.
The old guys stopped to play, they have family, children and are in real life battle now. ;-)
Our crew is more than friends and members: they are brothers. A "Full" doesn't fight with another "Full". Together, they learn.
A training is nothing more than a familiar meeting.

Loyalty, friendship and respect: pillars that supports "Full Connect Quake Clan" and guides the ways of its members.
