[[File:Logo_mk.JPG|thumb|[MK] Quake clan]] During the heyday of Quake, many clans were created, playing Quake on the Internet. The servers were always filled with players. Quake became a religion. But time passes, the Quake movement gradually dies. And the remaining players - those who remain faithful to Quake, unite again.<br> This was the case with the Moscow Quake Forces and KGB clans. At first, they had a large number of players, but gradually almost all of them became "dead souls". And then the remaining best players of these clans united under a new name: MOSCOW KEEPERS.<br> The MOSCOW KEEPERS clan was formed in July 1998 by players of the MQF clan: Gleb, Wonavi, GyperLexa and the KGB clan: RuLeZzZ and RiPpEr. After some time, AlexKiller, Chikatilo and RL from the PV clan joined them.<br> The name was chosen for a long time. First, they came up with the abbreviation MK, then, at the suggestion of WoNAvI, the abbreviation MOSCOW KEEPERS was adopted, which we understand as "Keepers". Keepers of the traditions of Quake.
