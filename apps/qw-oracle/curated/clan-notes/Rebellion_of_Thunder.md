=== History === Rebellion Of Thunder, aka ROT, is a Portuguese clan from Madeira Island. <br /> Founded in October 1997 by its fearless leader Ztranger and co-founded by ReWoP and Lord_n, the clan was the elite of the Portuguese QuakeWorld scene, defeating any opponents that would stand between them and the crown. <br /> The team was originally formed by Ztranger, ReWoP, Lord_n and M4sT3r. <br /> On April 9th, 1999, the clan was registered in IRC #PTnet. <br /> Players rarely played outside local servers until 1999, when ISDN/Cable arrived, so the community has always been turned to itself. <br /> ROT biggest rival was Team Defs, which was never able to dethrone The Rebellion. <br /> High pings were a big issue for Portuguese players, so from 2003 forward, in order to be able to participate in European tournaments with better pings, the team added international players. <br /> The clan participated in a few international events but mainly Portuguese ones, until it dies in 2008. <br /> Hammer runs the clan from 2018 to 2025 and takes it to the highest division and scores this team has ever accomplished. <br /> LONG LIVE THE REBELLION !!
