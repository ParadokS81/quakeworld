## History

Founded by Unabomber (Contra's brother) RS Terminators stands for "'Rio Grande do Sul' Terminators" originally compounded by members from this brazillian state. Being one of the oldest brazillian clans around RST have archived many victories through the years. The clan has a really strong and consistent team, and many other good brazillian players have been members of RST.
