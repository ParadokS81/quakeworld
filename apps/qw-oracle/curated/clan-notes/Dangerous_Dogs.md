## History

The clan originally formed with Bandog, Pitbull, Grego, Replicant and Lord Jonny. They played one full season in Div 2 and managed to win it quite convincingly. However once they were placed in Div 1 the clan disbanded after a few games as some players did not want to play quakeworld so seriously :DD

## Achievements


Came top of Div 2 of Wireplay league. Disbanded shortly after.
