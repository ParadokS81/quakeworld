## History

In its prime n27 enjoyed good success against top Finnish and Swedish clans. This was largely due to the fact that n27 had an appartment where one room was dedicated to clan gaming and most active members would come there to play. The gaming room allowed seamless mm3 communication and game coordination. In addition n27 had the prodigy player Vana and leader Epic to count on. 

Unfortunately n27 became inactive soon after the gaming room was no longer available due to people moving to different cities and as such the full potential of n27 was never realized in a tournament or league, just separate official games.

## Achievements

* 2002 - 1st place: Cession LAN in Espoo, Finland.
