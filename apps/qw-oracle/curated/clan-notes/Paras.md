Paras is a Finnish QuakeWorld clan founded in 2009.

## History

The clan Paras was founded in autumn 2009 by Panjabi and Heag. EQL10 Division 4 was the first tournament where the clan entered.. and somehow achieved 3rd place.

Paras means ''[http://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=bestest bestest]'' in English.

## Achievements

* Participated in EQL 10 Division 4. Achieved 3rd place.
* Participated in EQL 11 Division 3
* Participated in EQL 12 Division 3, qualified for the playoffs
* Participated in EQL 13 Division 3, qualified for the playoffs
* Participated in EQL 14 Division 3
* Participated in EQL 16 Division 2. Achieved 2nd place.
