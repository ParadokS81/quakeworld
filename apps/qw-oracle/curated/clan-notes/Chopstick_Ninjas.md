Between the known and the unknown lies the chopstick ninjas.

## History

A Danish Quake clan, made from irl friends in 1997, before they were even online. Started up again again in 2010 by some new friends.

## Achievements

* 2003 - 1st place: NQR Season 4 Division 2
* 2003 - 4th place: NQR Season 5 Division 2
* 2004 - 9th plce (tie): NQR Season 6 Gold Cup (Match report of Eighth Final)
