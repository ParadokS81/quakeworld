## History

The Dark Icemarines were founded in 1998 by the three school friends DarkWarrior, !ceman (now RaptoR) and Maestro from Mannheim, Germany. The name derives from the two clan leaders (DARKwarrior and ICEman). 
In it's teamplay history, some of the big successes would be the 1. place in the English UKCL for two times and the 2. place in the German IGL. 
Many members from all around Europe joined und left the clan. Some of the more known ones are for example Reppie and Blixem from the Netherlands and Venture from Germany.

Nowadays the clan is inactive and you can find many of the former members playing in other clans.
