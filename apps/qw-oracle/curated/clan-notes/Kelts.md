## History

Kelts started in 2010 by z0mbie90 and NukeProof as a clan for new and inexperienced players to have some fun and get some games going. One of the things they did was to have n00b4on4 nights where all the new and inexperienced players where welcomed to join and practice. They also got some of the better players in the game to come and join to give some pointers and tips for the players.
It didn't last long, just after a few n00b4on4's most of the players had moved on to other clans or stopped playing the game.
