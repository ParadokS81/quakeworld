A 4on4 clan consisting of HPW's that populated the servers back in 1999 or so when Quakeworld was still at its prime.

## History

Divide et Impera was founded by Alpha 1 (aka Åke Vader) and his cousin Z-Q-03 (aka Björta) after having spent several hours on the FZ gibfest servers and wanted to try out the action in 4on4. DEI always had a hard time winning games and some of their opponents were Quake Undertakers, NoNS and SPLATT which never offered easy resistance. The first four players were Alpha 1, Z-Q-03, Quid and Vulcanizer. After a while Necropulse (now Pulsen) was added to the roster which was seen as a good recruitment.
