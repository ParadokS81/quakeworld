Somewhat defunct Australian clan.

## History

In 2004, mustapha and Mortuary played a lot of 2on2. They wore a blue LED before their names and were effectively unstoppable on DM6. There was a two week LAN in there somewhere. Considering both their names started with "m" they decided they should come up with a name that encompassed the fact. '''Memento Mori''' was settled on and the two soon recruited newcomer of about 5-6 months, Umulamahri, in whom they saw massive talent. Friends Owl, thermal and psikaar soon joined their ranks and the lineup was complete.
