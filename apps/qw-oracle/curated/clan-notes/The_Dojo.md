The Dojo is a North American training ground for new and lesser-experienced QW players.

## History

'''The Dojo''' was founded by Cyan and Myth with two students, Silas and Proz. Although the Dojo was not created until 2006, numerous North American QW players have received training from Myth, Cyan, and KovaaK.
