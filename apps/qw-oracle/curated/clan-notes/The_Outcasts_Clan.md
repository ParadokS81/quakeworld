## History

Established by LethalWiz and Lob0 in 1996, TOC played deathmatch 4on4 (HPW) until mid-1997 when  they decided to focus on CTF with their new clan The Millenium Outcasts.

So in the spring of 2006 LethalWiz and Lob0 decided to try a comeback in the qw-scene after having played other games like Counter-strike and Quake3.
