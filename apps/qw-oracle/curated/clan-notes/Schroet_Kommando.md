Legendary Quake clan from Oberhausen, Germany. Formed by three brothers - Kane, Griff and Burke.

## History

Started out as a QW clan. Today Schroet Kommando, SK, SK-Gaming, is the worlds leading multi-gaming clan
with several squads in different games. Their homepage is also the biggest e-Sport portal
in the world.
