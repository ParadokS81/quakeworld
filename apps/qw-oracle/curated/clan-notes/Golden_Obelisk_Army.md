## History

The GOA clan was formed on May 4, 1998. This was the time when Expert and KUBatura were still lamers and learned to play online. Despite this, the clan performed quite well in two wars, although it lost. Within four months, several strong, promising players were accepted, who showed themselves well in matches with MTK and MC. A little later, it was decided to organize Q2 and TF departments of the clan, which received their commanders and roster.
