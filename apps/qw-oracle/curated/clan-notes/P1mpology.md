## History

In 2004 some guys tried to reanimate the German QuakeWorld scene by starting the GEQL Season 1, a German clan league, to bring back old clans and perhaps even create some new ones. At that time, the guys from P1mpology were playing Quake 3, but decided to install QuakeWorld to be able to play "the mother of all games" and compete versus some notorious QuakeWorld clans such as Schroet Kommando and Imperial Bounty Hunters. Then clan went inactive again after the season but never offcially disbanded.
