## History

Thangorodrim was founded at January 2003. Samgorod returned to Quakeworld and wanted to create a brand new clan with a unique name.
J.R.R. Tolkien's Silmarillion was Samgorod's favorite book at the day and Thangorodrim (Morgoth's Mountain of Fire) sounded a good name for the clan.
First Samgorod recruited a few old friends (as a veteran it was easy) and so {T} started practising for NQR4. After a while we were fortunate to cross paths
with some fine players and so our ranks grew constantly stronger. We tasted some glory and suffered some bitter defeats,
but most importantly we had a lot of fun.

## Achievements

* 2003 - 1st place: Clan Base QuakeWorld DM Cup Fall Third League Group B
* 2003 - 2nd place: NQR Season 4 Division 6
* 2003 - 4th place: NQR Season 5 Division 4
* 2004 - 2nd place: NQR Season 7 Division 3A
* 2004 - 7th place: NQR Season 8 Division 3B
