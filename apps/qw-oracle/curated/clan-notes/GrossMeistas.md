"Die GrossMeistas" were, beside 5:54 and Gib Disco, one of the first three Quake Clans founded in Austria.

## History

Founded in early 1996 by Kautzy and DrBrain on the way home (in a tramway) from a "Doom 2" Lanparty in Vienna. The GrossMeistas were featured in an Austrian TV show later in 1998 which might have been the very first documentary about Quake to be shown on television.
