## History

It all started when Fog (FoggeR) and I set up a network on 2 computers a year ago and started dueling on it periodically. The idea of ​​creating a clan had not yet formed in our heads. And we didn't know about such a thing until I ([RD-Y]ToXeR) got the Internet (March 1998). At first, FFA was a novelty for us, but then, of course, it became the norm :) And one night I was sitting alone on a deserted server and someone named Caribbean came there... :) We hung out, became friends and then the idea of ​​creating a clan became an interesting alternative for us, and our old (since school) buddy BuL appeared on the Internet. And if Caribou's memory serves me right (it certainly fails me), the clan with the euphonious :) name Radiation DecaY was formed on May 18, 1998. We played together on servers for quite a long time, until summer came and the full camping season began, in the sense of the word, that is, everyone began to constantly go away, leave, and temporarily our clan went into hibernation ) But this did not prevent us from recruiting a new member under the nickname Gys, who after joining our ranks went somewhere :) But autumn has almost arrived and I can't wait for the moment when we can all get together and finally work on this ubiquitous teamplay...
