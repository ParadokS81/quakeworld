on E1M2 in AFITD, July 1998]] on E3M2 in AFITD, July 1998]] Norwegian old school Quake clan with players using fake nicks or aliases. These types of clans, featuring players who for the most part had other clans and played with aliases, emerged in 1997. An explanation could be that the major Norwegian clans at that time attracted so many players they simply became too big to give everyone enough game time. Thus, fake nick clans like Mikke Mus Klan, Ernie Killers and Rectum Intruders could have been an opportunity for players to finally get some 4on4 action.
