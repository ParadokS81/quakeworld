  STASI was a QuakeWorld clan founded by the leaders Marx and jENs of the former RealQuake clan Chaos from Kreml in late 1997. One year later STASI was put to rest by Nikita and Marx when both players left the clan to join Beasts of Deletion. In 1999 the LPB-clan Spartacus was founded by Marx (Ubermann), Nikita, RinceWind (Angua) and Daver and STASI got dusted off as the HPW-division of Spartacus. Among the players in the refounded STASI was the danish player Warrior and swedish players Xorgal and Kask (Trinidy). During the two years the clan was active STASI played over 60 official games and had over 30 listed players.
