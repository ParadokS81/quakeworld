## History

This team only played four games in EQL Season 2 after they went inactive again. The team featured Goljat who had played in Slackers but this was the first season that Slackers didn't play in years. While ParadokS decided to play with Russians in Death Team, Goljat was in this team which also featured two other ex-Slackers', Champ and Slebe.
