## History

After having played Duke Nukem 3D and whatnot, Image:flag_fi.gif XoRoHo told Image:flag_fi.gif Sihvo about QuakeWorld some day during the lesson break. So they tried it out with ping 500 and decided that it was great fun. Soon later, Image:flag_fi.gif Juntti became interested as well and so these three fellas decided to form Hyvät, Pahat ja Rumat.
