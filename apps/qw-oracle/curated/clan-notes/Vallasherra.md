## History

The clan was founded by Image:flag_fi.gif Lenin and Image:flag_fi.gif Maniac after both players refused to join Finnish Famous Gunners. The clan was mostly playing Team Fortress and went inactive when the majority of the clan's players 'moved on' to play Counterstrike in 1999.
