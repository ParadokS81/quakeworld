## History


Evil Geniuses had a lot of members over the years. In the early days, many people played NetQuake exclusively, practicing with the heavyweight likes of Xtreme Prejudice, POSTAL, Legends, before the team went exclusively to QuakeWorld, where the team really established itself as one of the best. 


The team continued on to Quake 3, competing in the Quake Invitational League 3 (QIL 3) tournament, before fading out of the Quake scene.
