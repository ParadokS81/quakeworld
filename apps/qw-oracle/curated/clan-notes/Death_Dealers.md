Death Dealers the most notorious Polish QuakeWorld clan. === History === Death Dealers (D2) is a Polish QuakeWorld clan formed in 1998 by members of the two best Polish clans at the time being - Brotherhood of the Herb (BoH) and The Immortals (IM). Death Dealers was the first Polish clan known of having two separate divisions: LPB (division leaders: Acid/Dread) and HPB (division leader Plast). The clan was mostly inactive in 2002-2003, until Plast's return and reactivation in 2004. Since then D2 has participated in almost every European league, including NQR and EQL, until the clan disbanded again in 2007.
