## History

This team only played for a short amount of time after hlt left Enigma and before Clan Malfunction came back together.
In Spring 2010, EQL Season 11, Hlt, Darkki, Bandit and Dmt played all games, Mazer was on their lineup but didn't play a single map, they finished and seventh in division 1, qualifying for the playoffs.
In Fall 2010, EQL Season 12, all of the players on their lineup played some maps. They qualified as fifth for the division 1 playoffs.
