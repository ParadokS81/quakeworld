## History

Founded by Laxr, Scratchy and carapace, the name comes from an Nirvana song off the Nevermind album. After Laxr and Scratchy left to join GamBlers, carapace took the role as clan leader with cero as a co-leader.

* 2003 - 3rd place (tie): QHLAN 5 4on4 tournament
