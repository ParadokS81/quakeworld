Very briefly about us. The clan was formed in September 1997. At first, it consisted of only two people - littleB and Invisible. After the All-Russian Quake Championship in St. Petersburg, where they took quite high places, StandartQuake began to slowly give up its positions to TeamFortressy, and so the idea of ​​​​assembling a real team appeared. Knight was the first to be accepted. After holding several grids in an ordinary small St. Petersburg apartment (on which we managed to fit enough people to play 6x6 in TF, while there was also a dedicated server in the form of a mother with a separately connected power supply and without a monitor), Kash (the most elite demoman-defender) joined us - the very rock of the clan, thanks to which we can not worry about our flag. The next was Shooter, who most often plays as an engineer, since only he can simultaneously repair the gun at the flag and =) tear apart the enemy's defense. At that time, only littleB went on the attack, and Muzzy ("Universal soldier") was sent to help him, and after that the attack became simply armor-piercing =). For some time, MasteR was on the team, but after he left, the search for a new player began. It was NarK, yes, that same NarK who took first place in the aforementioned championship, with whom our team became truly stellar. And then for some reason he left =( And we took Reset.
