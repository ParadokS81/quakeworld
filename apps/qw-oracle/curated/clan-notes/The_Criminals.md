## History

The Criminals clan was created in 1997 by Tomi. Back in the days it was one of the best Polish QuakeWorld clans, doing good especially on Dm2 and Dm6 in 2on2 tournaments. They were organizers of Polska Liga Klanow (PLK) [Polish Clan League] and the clan was participating in multiple leagues such as D4, PLGS, Villains, ALGS among other.
