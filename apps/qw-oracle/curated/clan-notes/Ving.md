VING is a {{Flag/se}} 4on4 clan created in 2006. The team consists of three brothers (Mythic, Mille & Edvin) and their irl friend Sailorman. Ving is short for Vingåker, our home town in Sweden. It all started as a way to play together in between games with our original clans. We played together vs. div1 teams and mixes just for fun back in the days. During this time we chose the color "8 8" because it was the ugliest one we could find that was not taken yet. When we all started playing again in 2025 we decided to play together for real this time, and recently we decided to spice up our colors and changed them to "12 3". We also changed the team name in game to "0151" which is our area code.
