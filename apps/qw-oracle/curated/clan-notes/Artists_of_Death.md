Austrias most successful Quakeworld Clan.

## History

Founded soon after qtest was released Artists of Death have been the third clan in Austria and the 57th Worldwide. In their prime Artists of Death were the best Austrian Quake Clan finishing 3rd in the DeCL Season 4 and reaching the "never played playoffs" of the controversial DeCL Season 5.

## Achievements

* 1999 - 3rd place: DeCL Season 4
* 2000 - Playoffs: DeCL Season 5
