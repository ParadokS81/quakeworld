Symphonies of Sickness [SS] clan - was formed by three former members of the Russian Players - Raiders clan, more specifically, they were Carcass, Doom and BigFoot (Mr. LoM joined us a little later, about half an hour after the formation:))) ). This significant event happened in the middle of October 1998, since the exact date is unknown... we can assume that it happened on the 15th. The middle, so to speak:) On February 23, 1999, the decision was finally made to merge the Black Legion and Symphonies of Sickness clans. As a result, SS became 4 more people, namely, DeathKnight, MMX, Drakkus and ALX joined us. In the spring of 1999, MMX left the clan. In the summer of 1999, after our first teamplay championship, two more good players joined us. Gladiator and White-Hot from the RDY clan. At the same time, ALX was expelled as he had never participated in the life of the clan. The name was chosen specifically to coincide with the abbreviation of the elite unit of the imperial army of Nazi Germany, but only for fun :) We are not supporters or opponents of Nazism, we just do not relate to it in any way :) At the moment, the form of government in the clan is democracy (I think... maybe I'm wrong). The clan plays _only_ on LAN, although some (we will not point this out... it does not matter what) prefer QW due to their small ping. In any case, official clan wars are held exclusively on LAN in one of the Moscow clubs. Wishes, challenges to wars, and in general anything you want can be sent to any of the clan members via mail or ICQ, contact addresses can be found by clicking on the nickname of the person you need. (с) Carcass
