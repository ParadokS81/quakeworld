## History


Founded 1999 by Syxx, rayRO, SlyfoX and ''Saruman''. This clan was not only an QuakeWorld clan. With around 30 Members this Clan played together with the famous ''Hank-cLAN'' in alliance different LAN Games like Unreal Tournement, Quake 2 Action Quake or Strategy Games like Age of Empires, etc... The Quakeworld Section was just the old Team of the Clan Quake Guardians.
