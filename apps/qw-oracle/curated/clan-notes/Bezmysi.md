Bezmysi is a 2on2 team formed for the occasion of the NNQL tournament.

## History

History of the name goes deep into Flamer's progamer PC tweaking while he tried orthodox method where you play without using the mouse. The phrase "without the mouse" translates as "bez mysi" into Czech language. And so he changed his IRC nickname while doing this tweaking to Flamer|BEZMYSI. Some of his teammates from the Chosen clan were absolutely smashed by this. The effect of reading the "bezmysi" word was very brutal for them.

The brain devastating effect sounded promising so we've selected it as the name of the team.
