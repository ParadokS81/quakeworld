, 1997]] , 1998]] Clan consisting almost solely of fake nickers, though it appears Effie decided to keep the name. These types of clans, featuring players who already had other clans but played with aliases, emerged in 1997. An explanation could be that the major Norwegian clans at that time attracted so many players they simply became too big to give everyone enough game time. Thus, fake nick clans like Ernie Killers, Mikke Mus Klan and Rectum Intruders could have been an opportunity for players to finally get some 4on4 action.
