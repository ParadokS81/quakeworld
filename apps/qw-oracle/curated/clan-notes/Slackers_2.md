Second team founded by Slackers players ParadokS and Murdoc.

## History

The plan of creating a second team had been on the Image:flag_eu.gif Slackers table for quite a long time. The initial idea was to gather active new players that would form a division five team. There was something like an application form and somewhat of a site made by Murdoc. It was well over a year later, maybe even two, when things started to roll again. 

It was quickly decided that forming a division five team would need more time than they had, the plan changed to create a division two team with players that had potential to grow into division one. It didn't take too much time to get Image:flag_pl.gif Cpe, Image:flag_nl.gif Mja, Image:flag_se.gif TheEvilDog and Image:flag_hu.gif Zero. It seemed wise to add a player from Image:flag_eu.gif Slackers one to coach while being on the team itself and since the first team had enough members since Image:flag_se.gif Pascal joined Image:flag_se.gif Krab teamed up with Image:flag_eu.gif Slackers 2.

EQL Season 5 was the first tournament that the team entered and it went fairly well. For different reasons during the season Image:flag_se.gif Åke Vader joined the ranks. After ending third in the regular season the team made the finals of division two where they faced Image:flag_pl.gif Death Dealers. Having a degraded lineup, missing Åke Vader and Krab and thus playing with Zero, TheEvilDog, Mja and Cpe, the team couldn't reach the level of playing they usually did. On the first two maps, dm3 and e1m2, Zero played very good and thus the team took a 2-0 lead. After this Death Dealers, who played with Image:flag_pl.gif Avenger, Image:flag_england.gif Serox, Image:flag_england.gif Keyser, Image:flag_se.gif Moltas, Image:flag_pl.gif Plast and Image:flag_pl.gif Blasphemer, won the next three maps and thus the division 2 final.

After the season summer kicked in and about a month before the regular season Image:flag_se.gif Martin joined Slackers. Image:flag_se.gif Åke Vader had left due to  inactivity and there had been little to no pracs. At this stage a new idea came up, creating two even division one teams. The initial idea was to have Image:flag_dk.gif ParadokS, Image:flag_nl.gif Murdoc, Image:flag_pl.gif Insane and Image:flag_se.gif Krab in one team and Image:flag_se.gif Martin, Image:flag_se.gif Pascal, Image:flag_nl.gif Dragon and Image:flag_hu.gif Zero in the other. After a few weeks and not too many pracs from both teams Image:flag_se.gif Martin and Image:flag_se.gif Pascal decided to revive Fragomatic.

After this event things turned back to the old situation of having SR1 and SR2. Since Slackers 2 got promoted to division one and were struggling a bit it was decided they needed some recruits. The first one to join was Image:flag_nl.gif Purity, Image:flag_nl.gif Dragon had been lobbying for ages to get him in Slackers itself but it never happened. Slackers 2 was less of a problem but even with the new player the team still wasn't ready for division one and after some serious talking skills by ParadokS the old Image:flag_se.gif Lege Artis player (just like Purity playing in Freedom the season before) Image:flag_se.gif Striker joined and the team was complete.

EQL Season 6 turned out to have two sides, at the start of the season everybody was active, Image:flag_se.gif Sassa was an admin and said the playoffs would be held on QHLAN 11, then the site went down and nothing happened before the new year had started and someone else picked up the slack. Without a site playoffs were held, Slackers 2 had played maybe two maps in a few months time and Image:flag_se.gif Striker was nowhere to be found. Still they managed to win an e1m2 against the later winners Image:flag_fi.gif The Viper Squad in the semifinal.

The season after activity wasn't high enough to keep two teams and both merged. The then former second team players Image:flag_hu.gif zero and Image:flag_se.gif krab became regulars in the starting lineup from EQL Season 7 onwards, zero remains active and a starter in the first team of Slackers to this day. Other ex-Slackers 2 players Image:flag_nl.gif Mja and Purity made official appereances for the first team a few times per season between EQL7 and EQL11 including the EQL10 semifinal against Image:flag_se.gif Fragomatic. Ex-SR2 player Image:flag_se.gif TheEvilDog played for the first team in EQL10 and EQL11 group games. Krab took a step back in EQL Season 12 when Image:flag_se.gif En_karl joined the first team, when ex-Slackers and Slackers 2 player Image:flag_se.gif Striker also joined he even became number 6 during this season. When En_karl left again the season after, EQL Season 13, he was pretty active as number 5 again as Striker took a spot in the first four. 

A few years after the second team merged with the first, in december 2010, Slackers decided to revive this project. Ironicly the first Slackers team featured a lot of old Slackers 2 players since Image:flag_se.gif Striker, who didn't play since he quit while he was in Slackers II rejoined Slackers shortly before the revival of the seacond team. Image:flag_hu.gif Zero was still a starting player and also Image:flag_se.gif krab was active with the first team as their fifth player. The new Slackers II team played their first games together in the EQL Pro qualifications, where they were unable to qualify being a fresh team who didn't play together at all before the qualification started. The players they fielded were Image:flag_se.gif Phrenic, Image:flag_dk.gif CrazyMac, Image:flag_fi.gif Niomic, Image:flag_fi.gif Jebbis, Image:flag_pl.gif Kloze and Image:flag_fi.gif Bandit. As Image:flag_dk.gif CrazyMac only played the qualification because Image:flag_dk.gif ParadokS asked him to and he didn't intend to stay with the team, Image:flag_dk.gif Niw took his spot in the new lineup.

With this lineup they entered EQL Season 13 and played in division 1. Slackers II missed the playoffs due to some controversial decisions by the admin team. Besides the fact that the points system was a bit weird (flawed in some peoples eyes), meaning that SR2 with 4 games won missed playoffs while TKS made it while they only won two games (but lost more often with 1-2). The controversy was that TKS was allowed to play DC after the regular season ended even though this season intentionally didn't feature a catch up week and also that the admins allowed them to play both games (against the same opponent) on the same day (this wasn't allowed according to the rules). This led to some drama and TKS passing SR2 on the standings after the last day of the regular season, knocking SR2 out of the playoffs.

After this season the team was rebuild, Image:flag_fi.gif Bandit and Image:flag_fi.gif Moje were asked to leave. Image:flag_se.gif Phrenic decided to try his luck elsewhere and joined the new team Image:flag_se.gif Sublime. Image:flag_se.gif Krab went back to lead the second team since he didn't get much playing time in the first team and Image:flag_se.gif TheEvilDog rejoined the second team, they formed the new team with Image:flag_dk.gif niw and Image:flag_pl.gif Kloze who stayed with the team. Image:flag_fi.gif niomic is expected to come back after summer when the competitions start again.
