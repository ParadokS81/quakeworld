## History

One of the original wireplay quakeworld clans. Famous for their great attitude.

Confusingly, there were two clans called SAS in the UK.

The other was started later by BestofBritish and Aerotus.

SaS despite being a wireplay only clan is probably better remembered. 

Very active all through 1998, there was never a night when they wouldn't have enough players for a scrimmage.

Defeated JAMS in a memorable match in WPDML Season 1, but finished behind them in the league, in fourth place overall.
