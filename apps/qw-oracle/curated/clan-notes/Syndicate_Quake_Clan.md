a german Quake and Quakeworld clan

## History

The Syndicate Quake Clan was formed by a small group of reallife friends, all coming from the small east-german town Erfurt. They never made it to any importance competition-wise, but nevertheless are a still a bunch of good buddies.

Over the years we had lots of good battles with clan Pentagon, which also comes from Erfurt, until we decided to join forces in a more competitive try and formed E:rfurt A:llS:tars T:eam.
