## History

Quakeklan split up into 2 teams for EQL Season 7 (initial plans were 3 teams, but rookie-team, Quakeklan.b, went inactive): Quakeklan 1 and 2.

The EQL Season 7 lineup of the team featured a few members that won the NQR Season 11 Rookie Cup but they didn't play a single game in the competition. In EQL Season 8 the team only managed to play one game and didn't signup for another league afterwards as the members scattered to other teams.
