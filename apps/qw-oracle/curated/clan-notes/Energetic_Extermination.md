Energetic Extermination was an international amateur multigaming clan from Germany. Founded in 2000 by the gamers cekay and NeWsAgE, the clan has been active in several games like Tactical Ops, Unreal Tournament, Halo, Red Faction, Diablo II, Soldat and Black & White. Especially in the games Tactical Ops and Unreal Tournament, the clan participated in official matches of the EWL and the Gamestar League. In 2006 the clan get inactive for years. After the temporary dissolution of the clan in 2008, some members were active in the game Quake Live for a while.

## Achievements

* 2003 - 16th place: ''Gamestar League''
* 2005 - 23rd place: ''EWL''
