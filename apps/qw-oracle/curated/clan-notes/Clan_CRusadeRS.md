Clan CRusadeRS or [CRS] is one of the famous Quake Clans in Serbia.

## History

Quake Clan CRusadeRS started it's existence some time in 1998. It spawned out of Belgrade's local hacker scene. 
It's membership consisted mostly of members of a BBS called "Temple of DooM", where the local hackers gathered at the time. 
Officially, Quake Clan CRusadeRS was founded during Belgrade's first Quake 2 tournament, and it began as the Quake 2 clan mainly, later to evolve into a general Quake Clan.
