Spawn Clan was one of the first and most successful Norwegian Quake clans. It was dissolved at the end of 1997, when most of the players went on to new clans like Game Over, 7th Sphere and The New Breed. * Website: [http://web.archive.org/web/19990221110535/http://home.sol.no/~eskjellu/spawn/bst2.htm Old home page (working)] === History === Spawn Clan was founded in October 1996. They quickly established themselves as one of the top Norwegian clans, second only to Carnage Clan. Its first official clan match was a victory against Swedish Chefs. Spawn Clan had an active first year, but the activity declined when several of the clan's best players decided to merge with Carnage Clan and Insomnia and form the new clan Game Over in November 1997. The remaining members decided to dissolve Spawn Clan and formed new clans like The New Breed and 7th Sphere. [http://web.archive.org/web/19990129055336/http://home.sol.no/~eskjellu/spawn/story.htm More on the Spawn Clan history can be found here.] Spawn Clan arranged what was probably the first Quake-LAN-party in Norway in January 1997 in Oslo. [http://web.archive.org/web/19990129022506/http://home.sol.no/~eskjellu/spawn/seteclan.htm A report from the LAN can be found here.] One month later, founder Sandman was also heavily involved in setting up the first of a series of legendary AskerLANs together with BM, founder of The Vicious Vikings. From its start in October 1996 and until November 1997 Spawn Clan played [http://web.archive.org/web/19990129041517/http://home.sol.no/~eskjellu/spawn/stats.htm 39 clan matches] and only lost three of them.
