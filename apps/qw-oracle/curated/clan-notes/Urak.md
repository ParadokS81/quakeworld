## History

Urak got a name in NQR Season 4 when Riker played in the top division one team Disorder but got caught faking with this clan. This resulted in a ban which has been talked about a lot. They didn't play for years after that but when Lege Artis went inactive again after EQL Season 4 they revived with old Lege Artis members Riker and Inferno. A lot of the members played together before in teams like Lithium, Assassins, Disorder the earlier mentioned Lege Artis and ofcourse Urak itself.
