Average Div2 clan from Sweden.

## History

The clan was founded by Nopee, Edvin and Elguapo in November 2006. We were the last remains of Da Quaking Manjakks which had fallen a few weeks earlier. Nopee, Edvin and Elguapo felt like starting something new and fresh and after some days of brainstorming we came up with Core. The question was which players we were going to recruit...

## Achievements

* Runner-up in NQR Season 10 Silver Cup
* Semifinalist in EQL Season 5 Division 2
