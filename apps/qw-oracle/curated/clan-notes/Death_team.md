One of the most respected and succesful teams in Russia. Currently inactive.

## History

Founded in 1999-2000 by best players of clans ''Armageddon'' and ''Golden Obelisk Army'': Slam, Die_kreker, Bloodliker, Gleb and Camperoid. First name was "Armageddon Deep Town", renamed to "Death team" 06/07/2001.
