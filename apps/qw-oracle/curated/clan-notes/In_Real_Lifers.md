Nationality: Czech Founded: December 2005 Clan tag: IRL Homepage: [http://cqe.rytir.com/irl/ In Real Lifers] (external link)

## History


Purpose of founding of the clan was to get the best four active Czech Players in Winter 2005 and put them in a challenge against other European clans in a 4on4 tourney.

In the end of EQL Season 2 Shpeq wasn't able to participate in the tournament so the clan added MiXu to it's roster to play remaining matches.

Lack of time lead to low number of practice matches that resulted to last place in EQL Division 4.
