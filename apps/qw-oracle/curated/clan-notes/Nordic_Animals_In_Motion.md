Started out with Rikoll and Trygve as a 2on2-team in NNQL. In late '07, Naim was joined by Arnette, who had recently returned to QuakeWorld after about two years of absence. Subsequently, Naim decided to form a clan for NQR11. With Votary and Overdose having left EnemyQuad, IgggY and Antic no longer had a full team to participate in NQR11, resulting in their recruitment to Naim. The clan brought back numerous Norwegian quake players.
