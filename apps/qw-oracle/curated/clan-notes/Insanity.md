## History

Formed after Dutch Hope fell apart, this happened because of internal affairs which made it impossible for Purity to remain a member of the clan. With Sargon as leader the team played as Dutch representives in NQR division 1 during NQR Seasons 7 and 8, only foreigner being Wigorf. Then after more troubles Murdoc left the team and Insanity left it's Dutch roots with bringing in Insane, Riker, Raket and Serox. Sargon, got kicked after some time because he wasn't good enough a player according to the new members.

Being a mix team, it disbanded after just one season, a part of that caused by Riker who had troubles with losing due to bad teamplay.

* 2005 - 9th in NQR Season 8 Division 1
