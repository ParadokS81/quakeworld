CCCP is a Russian QuakeWorld clan. Due to the differences between the Cyrillic and Latin alphabet, the clan is actually called SSSR. Reunited in the early 20s and took part in a couple of tournaments.

## History

Clan was renamed from USSR to CCCP prior to EQL Season 11.
