A temporary clan formed for EQL Pro.

## History

After the Viper Squad decided not to join EQL Pro, a group of good players gathered to be able to play in the league and not to make it too easy for Fusion, Suddendeath and Slackers. This marked the 4on4 comeback for Fifi who hadn't played in a team since EQL Season 9 and after this tournament Clan Malfunction would return to EQL Season 13 with old members Fifi, Fix, Hlt and new recruit Darkki.
