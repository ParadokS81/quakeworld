## History

Severed Head played QuakeWorld 'til late 1999. In early 2000 they formed a Quake3 squad, which didn't last very long. The clan completely vanished a few months later. All members except for Enasni, Yourdeath and Vatikan slowly disappeared during 2000. Later, Yourdeath played in clan pOrn and was often seen on FFA servers until a few years ago. Enasni is the only of these players still active in 2007.
