## History

WAR, short for Wicked Assault Rangers, founded in 2004 by Skillah in the NetQuake scene. 

Most members came over from the NetQuake clan NR which died off inactivity. But also some new members signed up making the WAR clan more experienced in the art of 4on4. 

In NQR Season 7 they started playing serious but could not get enough practises or matches going to end up high in the tournement.
It was in NQR Season 8 they went home with the win off DIV 5B. The playoffs were never played though. 

At the end of 2005 the clan died because of inactivity among most of the players. Most players quited the game or went to QuakeWorld for more activity on the servers.
