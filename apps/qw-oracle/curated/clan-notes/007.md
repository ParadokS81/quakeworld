When Bootcamp (initiative owned by Doomie and company helping newcomers to start their QuakeWorld adventure by playing practice 4on4 Team Deathmatch games with coaches on voice) went inactive, in order to promote QW and attract less experienced players to TDM, record (who just returned after another quite long break) decided to form a new team consisting of Bootcampers, CPMA/Duel/FFA players and rusty QW boomers. Once disbanded majority of active players joined various Division Ninety Nine teams. 007 brand was actually the reactivation of very old Polish HPB clan and tribute to them. <br> Team 1 name = $0$0$7<br> Team 2 name = ^O^O^7
