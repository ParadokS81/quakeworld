## History

* Founded 1997 by Blaps & Cynips in Stockholm, Sweden.
* Sported the infamous ZOO Dancehall server from the Internet connection of The Department of Zoology, Stockholm University.
* The RQ-server was one of the last and most popular in Sweden when it had to shut down by September 2002 because of the immature actions by a player called Stefan (not steppa, but a noob with the same name). This is probably as close to a "real" end of RQ gaming as you can ever hope to pin-point.
