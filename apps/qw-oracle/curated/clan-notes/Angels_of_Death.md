One late rainy autumn evening in the year 1997 AD, in the blessed lands of the Internet, a man with the nickname AlexRed met a stranger named IMMORTAL. The world of Quake was still in primordial chaos, and so these two warriors decided to unite in the fight against evil forces. Thus the Clan was born. They called themselves Angels of Death, and to this day the infidels tremble with holy horror, hearing about the Angels of Death!.. No one remembers the exact date of the Clan's creation, but the moment of its foundation is considered to be the time when the Clan Page appeared. This holy scripture was presented to the world at the end of the troubled month of October 1997. In those ancient times, there were still very few, less than a dozen, Russian Clans, and the Clan of the Angels of Death took a worthy place among them. The Clan grew quickly, accepting more and more new warriors into its ranks. The first to appear on this list were MEPhIstO and VovaKiller. These valiant people stand at the very foundation of the Clan, and deservedly occupy an honorable place among the Veterans, the oldest, most experienced and wise warriors of the Clan. There is no point in listing all the fighters, for they are all Brothers, and each of them is an integral link in the chain, the loss of which is difficult and almost irreparable for the Clan. The book about all the warriors of the Clan is set out below... There is also no point in listing all the Wars that our clan has been through. We have celebrated many glorious victories, and the Clan has tasted the bitterness of defeat. However, the Clan honors its Enemies, and hatred on the battlefield turns into fraternization after the battle. And if you are reading this manuscript, it means that the Clan is Alive! And in spite of all the dark forces of this damned world, he will live, and the Angels of Death will still dance on the corpses of their Enemies!..
