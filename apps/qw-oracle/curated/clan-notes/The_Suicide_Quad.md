Swedish clan founded in 2022 with former members of Oblivion, Lege Artis, Tribe of Tjernobyl, Chosen and Hellfire. In the first year of existence tSQ played {{team|The Axemen|flag=no}} a whopping 130 times with 66 wins and 64 losses. They bested them in the division 2 Grand Final of Qlan War Tournament 2 in a thrilling 3 - 2 victory for the Gold.
