## History

In the early days of Quake, when you still had NetQuake, a group of familair people started up Murderers and Demolishers, in the years to come it died and got resurrected many times, lived many fruitless years with no chance of succes. These days it's just a group who play together when online, whether it is QuakeWorld or any other game the MaD prefix is placed again.
