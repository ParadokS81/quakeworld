## History

Clan Cute was originally a Finnish Deathmatch clan and later started a CTF section by aquiring the Swedish CTF clan Section. Some early CTF members were Bullet, Concept, Space, Unit, Charon and LethalWiz. Later several other members were invited for example Inco, Pidde, Cadawer, Goose, WoW, Caveron and Capten.
