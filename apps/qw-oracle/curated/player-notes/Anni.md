## Information

Admin of German QuakeWorld Scene, Ownage and Ignition.

With an age of twelve (1989) I got my first PC and went through all the PC gaming titles at that time until today.
I spent more than a decade with the Quake Series, where Quakeworld captured me the most.
As a compensation to fast games, I also like to play relaxed games like Diablo 3 and Path of Exile.

I`m not a pro gamer, I die a lot and I stream for fun.
