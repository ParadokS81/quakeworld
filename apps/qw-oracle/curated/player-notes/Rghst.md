Rghst, a tough nick for all quakers to pronounce. He is a player from Germany that absolutely loves the 4on4 scene. His favourite and only good duel duel map is ztndm3.

## Trivia

* Not even Rghst himself can pronounce his own nick. His twitch username is ''ergehaeste''. Jehar has called him ''right ghost'' and ''radaghast''. At QHLAN2026, Milton picked him in his 4v4 draft team as ''letters''. The debate still goes on.
* Apparently, both him and Dopeskillz were once called Heinz - a conflict resolved when Dopi beat him in a duel for the prized title of ''Best Heinz EU''.
