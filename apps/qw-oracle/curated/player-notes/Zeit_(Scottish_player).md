BIO Compensating for his lack of Quake ability with an infectious enthusiasm, Zeit can usually be discovered wandering around the hallowed halls of the Church of Pent in a onesie with coffee in hand. Quickly introduced to Quakeworld by Ragga upon his joining Terror Byte Gaming, he soon picked up the basics and threw himself into the game with reckless abandon, mainly participating in their Quake Sundays FFA sessions, although has since ventured into duelling and more recently 2on2 with m00.
