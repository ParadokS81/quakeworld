Mateusz "Betowen" F is an oldschool Polish QuakeWorld player. From 1997 he played QW mainly on dial-up internet connection and with ping between 200-300 he had a great time. In 1998 Haggard asked him to join Hard Guns Crew and along with Qrush, Shadow and Insane played in very first and oldest HGC 4on4 squad. Because Hard Guns Crew got deactivated in 2000, Highlander asked him to join Demolition Crew. By the end of 2002 he completely retired from playing QW and after over 20 years without any further practice he appeared in Radomsko LAN Party QW LAN Party Poland 2024. During 2025 he tries to stay active and come back to Radomsko LAN Party in better shape.
