Started playign in FFA and TD2, but with very poor hardware. When he got an upgrade, he started to evolute in the game, and was invited to join Mom Wishes a Rocket by PolpaDaPaz. With the help of his clanmates of MwR, he started to increase his skill. After some time, he managed to be one of the best TD2 players in the brazilian scene, if not the best. In 2005 he had a short period playing CTF, but then moved to QuakeWorld, where he adapted very well. He was known for his POVDMM3 skills, with deadly LG. Still in 2005, he was invited to join Try Again by Devasso, and he decided to stick with them and make tAg one of the best. In 2009 the QuakeWorld Brazilian scene was very inactive. So he went to play other FPS, like [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsow_(video_game) Warsow], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quake_Live Quake Live] and [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-Strike:_Global_Offensive CS:GO]. When tAg resurged in QuakeWorld in 2018, he returned to the game.
