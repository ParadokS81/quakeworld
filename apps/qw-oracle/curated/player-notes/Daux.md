__NOTOC__ In his teens, without ways to buy Counter Strike or whatever, daux looked for free fps games on the internet. Luckily many were arena fps based on some Quake engine and eventually he found the original game. In 2016, scared by the complexity of 4on4 teamplay, he played mostly 1on1 on and off with similiar skilled players for a short while and left again in 2017. After seeing QHLAN2022 footage in 2023 he returned to QuakeWorld and 4on4 is not scary anymore and his favourite mode now! Favourite mode: 4on4 Favourite map: schloss
