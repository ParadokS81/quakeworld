Frost, formerly known as Koopa (not to be confused with the Irish player Koopa), is an active player currently playing out of Dubai. He is particularly known for his skills with the lightning gun on povdmm4. He started playing original quake in 2001. In 2005 formed with Stal1n the clan Impulse X. IX developed to be one of the strongest clans in the original quake brazilian scene. In 2006, when IX was closed, he moved to QuakeWorld and joined Animaniacs clan. He stayed with them until 2008. In 2009 he joined O Clan. He played with 6 (O Clan tag) only for 6 months, and decided to retire. Frost made a return in 2017, and rejoined his old clan Animaniacs. He took yet another brief break when moving to Dubai, but is now back fragging.
