Christian "Caveron"' Karlsson is an oldschool Quakeworld player who first started playing in 1997. He mostly played CTF but later also joined the Clan Arena guild Fast Forward. Caveron started playing in the CTF guild Necronomicon, NEC, and eventually moved on to OFF, H8, Oj! and finally Clan Cute where he played until he quit in 2003. Both Cute and Fast Forward were considered the top Swedish/European clans in their respective game mode at the time. Caveron started playing again in April 2024 after more than 20 years.
