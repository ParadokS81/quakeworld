From Challenge-TV's coverage of True Gamers Invitational: «Another player from whom much was expected before the tournament was Xenon, from Sweden. Regarded as a possible finalist, Xenon was definitely one of the most well-known players in the tournament. He is famous for being one of the best team strikers in the world, more so than as a 1-on-1 player. His performances with the famous Clan 9 have earned him Quake superstar status. I was not at all surprised to see that Xenon had the most style and cool of all of the players. Unfortunately, due to a host of problems including having to change computers at least 4 times and missing a wake-up call from the hotel, Xenon needed all of his cool and then some. He felt unlucky to lose to Columbo, but I think Columbo simply played better. From that point he knew his chances of beating Lakerman to get into the final four were slim, and he never played Lakerman. Xenon had some consolation in beating Reload convincingly on DM4, and in a closer game on DM6, but I'm sure he was overall disappointed with how the TGI turned out for him. Considering that 1-on-1 is not Xenon's natural game, I think he can certainly do well in the next tournament, should he keep at it. With his talents, Xenon has the potential to reach the top.»
