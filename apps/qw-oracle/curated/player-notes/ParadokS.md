Legendary Danish player famous for his aggressive gameplay on DM6 when dueling, as well as elaborate tactics and all-round skills in teamplay. Founded Slackers in 2001 together with Goljat and has been active in a lot of Quakeworld related projects such Duelmania, QuakeWorld.nu and live commentary of official matches.
