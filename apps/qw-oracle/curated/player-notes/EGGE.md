EGGE, also known as Geeg or ARTy, is an Australian gamer who kicked off his love of gaming after accidently wondering into a Quakeworld LAN back in Sydney in the late 90's. EGGE immediately fell in love with Quakeworld and went on to con his parents into buying his first PC, an Intel Celeron 300A and has been gaming competitively across multiple titles ever since. In 2021, he signed up for sdCup #1.

## Trivia

EGGE's youth was spent skateboarding at every chance he got and he went on to win a couple of local skate comps and managed to land his first job at the age in his early teens selling skateboards, snowboards and wakeboards out of a popular board shop in Sydney.

Dan's IRL nickname is ''eggy'' (even his own mother calls him egg!)

EGGE's naturally right handed however when it comes to gaming, he uses his mouse in his left hand.
