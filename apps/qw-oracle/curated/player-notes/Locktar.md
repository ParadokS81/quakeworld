{{DISPLAYTITLE:LocKtar}} Dennis "LocKtar" Larsson is a well known Quakeworld legend, maybe most renowned for his spectacular playstyle and energetic footwork, granting him the title "The Worlds Best dm2 player". A duel purist by heart, he can occasionally be observed lost in the mazes of dm3 (sng) in sporadic team games. As of 2016, together with {{player|carapace|flag=se}}, he formed {{player|Rune|flag=se}} to compete in various international 2on2 tournaments.
