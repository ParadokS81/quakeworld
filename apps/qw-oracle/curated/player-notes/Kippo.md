Tom "kippo" Grönholm is a Finnish QuakeWorld player. He has been active since Qtest was released back in February 24, 1996. Probably best known for playing 1on1 on the highest level for years, but he has never yet reached the very top. He has been representing Fraggers United well over 15 years as a team captain. Playing in a 2on2 team IPA together with Diki. "One of the most polite player in the finnish QW scene." (c) Vertigo [http://eql.quakeworld.nu/eql17/news/12 Interviewed] in 2013 by Hooraytio.
