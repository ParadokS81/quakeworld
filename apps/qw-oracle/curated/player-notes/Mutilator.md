Martin "Mutilator" Samuelsson is a former Quakeworld professional player. Belonging to the old guard, he was considered an all-rounder with impeccable knowledge of both 4on4 and 1on1. In 2006, he showed up for QHLAN 9 like jack-in-the-box and eventually beat {{Player|reppie|flag=nl}} in the Grand Final.
