Brief introduction. Played QW from 1997 to 2003, then moved on to play Return To Castle Wolfenstein for 6 years (Didn't play QW almost at all during that time). Played it in few top clans during those years and also in Finland's national team from 2007 to 2009. Retired from the battlefields of rtcw carrying home some regular tournament winnings and also a third place in 2007, second place in 2008 and finally a first place in 2009, with Team Finland in nations cup.
