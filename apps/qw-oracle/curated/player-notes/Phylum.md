Phylum is an Australian Quake player in Melbourne, Australia. Born a month before Quake 1 launched he has been gaming his entire life, picking up a mouse at age 2 to play Quake and Duke Nukem 3D. Between 2009 - 2013 he got his first taste of competitive online gaming in Quake Live, focusing mostly on FFA and Duel. In 2013 he started playing QuakeWorld and has been playing periodically since.
