{{DISPLAYTITLE:Speedball}} The French duel player Julien "Speedball" Corfmat arose in the later eras of QW, quickly earning a reputation as an aggressive and smart player. He discovered the game by watching DDE3, and been helped to levelup his skills with #qw.fr users and aero-rapers. Speedy is also a duel specialist but never reached the first place. He played only three 4on4 games in his life, but he really likes tdm when it is 2on2.
