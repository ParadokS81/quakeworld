Having StarCraft as her favourite game, Alice had only played one FPS (Overwatch) for a short time before eventually stumbling upon Quakeworld. Having realized Overwatch wasn't her cup of tea, she was recommended "The Original Quake" and quickly found it clean, smooth and elegant. Alice first started playing with {{player|DOG_SACRIFICE|flag=gb}}, then later binged up on Youtube QW videos to improve her game. Her favorite mode is 2on2 and she is a big fan of dm2.
