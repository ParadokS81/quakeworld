Jan "Peppe" Piechal is a Swedish Quakeworld player, perhaps more known as an admin and crew member of recent iterations of QHLAN. In recent years he's been spotted more and more as guest caster in various shows, when not streaming on [https://www.twitch.tv/Peppe_qw his own channel]. As a player Peppe fears no man, although his aggressive and sometimes mindless play style cost him games.
