A longstanding representative of the French Quake scene, Viag merely tolerated the switch to Quake 2 then 3, to which he much preferred the CPMA mod due to its heavy QuakeWorld inspiration. He moved to Montréal, QC in the early 2000s but can still be found on european servers.
