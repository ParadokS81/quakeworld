dot, also known as Murz1k, is a professional Quakeworld player from Saint Petersburg. Always in love with PC shooters, he found q3test in 1999 and later Quake 3 which he played up until 2001 when he fell for the fast and dynamic gameplay of QW FFA. In the wake of great Russian players like {{Player|doom|flag=ru}}, {{Player|unknown|flag=ru}} and {{Player|ded|flag=ru}}, dot slowly began practising to get to the highest levels.
