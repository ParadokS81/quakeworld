Snoozer was watching when {{Player|HarlsoM|flag=au}} and his mitten gloves won a tournament Sylvania, Sydney hosted by the 'deathNET lan group' in 1997. After that he thought "I have to play this game." Snoozer is an Australian QWTF player masquerading as a QWDM player. He spent most of his time over the last 23 years playing QWTF, however occasional QWDM with his friends {{Player|greyseer|flag=au}} and {{Player|ABC|flag=au}}. Recently he has found the active Australian QW community on discord and is enjoying the banter and open acceptance for all skill levels. He still misses his Software Quakeworld client. ====Play Style==== After getting over the shock of not spawning with a stack and weapons, zzz had to adjust his play style to avoid shooting walls, less +forward, and start to develop item control, and timing. He still he has a hard time not shooting walls and teammates as well as generally remembering what all the areas are called in maps, however is enjoying the journey.
