{{DISPLAYTITLE:Oscar*x*}} Oscar started playing Quake not long after his big brother Thor. Thor started playing at the age of 4. Oscar actually started playing at the age of just 3. Oscar liked the family atmosphere that surrounded the usual game setups. With different teams setup, although usually Oscar and Dad against Thor, they had a great little “thing” going in the highly adrenalized and bizarre magic world of Quake. Days never to forget. Oscar has attended both QHLAN2017 and Dreamhack Summer 2017 with Thor and their father.
