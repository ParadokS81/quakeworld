===Casting===
Hemostick's return to QuakeWorld in 2019 was significant for a scene where media content had been dwindling. He revitalized live coverage and quickly established himself as one of the scene's most important broadcasters.
Catering to all types of events, although primarily 2on2 and duel, he contributed to increased interest in QuakeWorld with his humorous commentary and engaging with viewers to provide an interactive experience.

Countless hours of competitive matches can be found in his Twitch archive.
