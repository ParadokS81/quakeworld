## Information

<b>Favorite maps: </b>dm4, schloss, ztndm3
<br><br>

Dutch player from Tilburg.<br>
Started playing Quake 1 back in 1996 and joined many LAN events between 1997-2003 at CyberGate (later dubbed as The Cybe), Europe's first internet/LAN café also located in Tilburg, NL.<br>
Mostly played QuakeWorld FFA and Q3A duels back then. Also Quake 2 and 3, as a member of the Quake 2 Weapons Factory clan Prisoners of War and the Q3A Instagib clan Sailor Scouts (USA). <br><br>
