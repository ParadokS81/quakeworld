Tomi is founder of The Criminals and one of the founders of Polska Liga Klanow (PLK) [Polish Clan League]. He has returned to QuakeWorld after ~20 years of inactivity. In 2023, he organized the 1on1 TEC ELITE CUP Tournament, which had its final at QHLAN-Stockholm in January 2024. https://www.quakeworld.nu/wiki/TEC_Elite_Cup_2023 - The plan for 2024 is to organize the TEC ELITE CUP - 4on4 Edition

## Trivia

TeC was one of the best clans in Poland when they were initially active between 1998 and 2003.
