Best Brazilian QW player from 2002 untill 2011, Doob (a.k.a. ric or db) holds the biggest amount of titles in Brazil's QuakeWorld history/scene. With a Q3 background since 1999, he started playing both games at 2001 but in 2002 he focused in QW only and started his successful track, carving his name in Quake scenario (worldwide I would say, since CHTV had a quite global impact at the time). Having Mur as his 2v2 partner, both were almost unbeatable for many years and it was one of the biggest partnership of brazilian QuakeWorld community. * Real name: Ric * Date of birth: 80's * Nationality: Brazilian * Current clan: O Clã
