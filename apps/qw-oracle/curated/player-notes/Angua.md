Angua is a notorious QuakeWorld player from Stockholm, Sweden - who's been playing since he was 12. He was an NQR admin and plays in top-clan Clan Malfunction. He is referred to by some as the "(un)official king of QuakeWorld movement".

## Trivia

* Started playing as 'Rincewind' but fake-nicked on QuakeWorld Team Fortress servers as 'Angua', which later became his "official" nickname.
* Both his regular nickname 'Angua' as well as his first nickname 'Rincewind' are taken from Terry Pratchett's '<i>Discworld</i>' series.[[#References|<sub>[1]</sub>]] Rincewind is a failed student of the '<i>Unseen University</i>' and spends most of his time running away from people that want to kill him.[[#References|<sub>[2]</sub>]] Angua (actually called 'Angua von Überwald') is a female werewolf working at the city watch of Ankh-Morpork.[[#References|<sub>[3]</sub>]]
