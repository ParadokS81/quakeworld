Dutch/Polish player that started playing QW in 1997, and had his most active peak from 2002 to approx. 2006 (mostly playing with Dutch players Murdoc, Dragon, Purity, Optimizer, Oase, Amen and the likes). Was most known for being an avid FFA player, and for his solid E1M2 4on4 plays. After a long hiatus, started playing more regularly again in 2025 by participating in QWSL DIV2.
