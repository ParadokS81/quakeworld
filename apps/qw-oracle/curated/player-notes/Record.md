An old-school Polish QuakeWorld player who started his journey in 1996 on LAN and soon after online as HPB (High Ping Bastard) on probably the world's worst dial-up connection. Since always he was playing mainly Team Deathmatch mixes/FFA/Duels and for different teams participating (or benchwarming) in Challenge Smackdown, Nations Quake Rank, European Quake League and several other tournaments and drafts. One of the top QWiki contributors. Former QWLANPL co-organizer and tournaments admin. He has attended few big QuakeWorld LAN parties such as M, QWLANPL and QHLAN.
