Bance is an American that started playing QuakeWorld in year 2024. While working on an AFPS inspired Half-Life mod he tried QuakeWorld and realized there was no point in making a new game since QuakeWorld is the peak of FPS gaming. Bance is well known for being the only tournament organizer in NA. He's still an up and coming duel and 2on2 player with rapidly improving results. What Bance lacks in skill he makes up for with his quick wit and trash talk. Most famously his ongoing banter with cronus. It is the general consensus of the NAQW community that Bance is owning cronus in the trash talk domain and soon will be owning him in duels.
