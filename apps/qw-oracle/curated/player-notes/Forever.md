Started playing in original Quake. Shortly after he moved to QuakeWorld, which became his favorite mod, because of its speed and physics. Already in QuakeWorld, he was invited by Marlboro and Legi (br) to join Lords of War. After some time with them, some of LoW members became inactive, and he left. He joined RS Terminators for a period. But he had interest into play with Quakers in Black, and was invited by Boblast to join. Unfortunately, just like in his LoW times, many QiB members went inactive, and he decided to left. Then he joined Pedreiro into forming Try Again, and the clan became one of the top at the time, together with O Clan and Animaniacs. After some period of inactivity, he got back in 2008 and joined Animaniacs, that still had many active members. But after 1 year he retired again. In 2018 he returned to Quake, and reformed Try Again with some of the old members.
