Seasoned QuakeWorld player Niclas "locust" categorizes as one of the Dragons of past times. Still bringing an unmatched and well-polished playstyle to the frontier, few are they who can out aim this beast. Prominent in duels, team games aren't equally polished. He only play 4on4 when someone carry him onto the battlefield. 2on2 though, is a mode more fitting. In recent years, he's found success together with bps as AeronautS, attending QuakeCon 2016 in Dallas and QHLAN2017 in Stockholm. 2020's Champ Champ - having won QuakeCon 2020 and Duelmania Reloaded 4 back to back - the most prestigious duel tournaments of 2020.
