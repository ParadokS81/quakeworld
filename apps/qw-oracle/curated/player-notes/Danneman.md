Danjel "Danneman" Ahnér (born August 30, 1983) is a friend of {{Player|razor|flag=se|link=Razor (Swedish Player)}}, and got drawn into the community by playing other games together, mostly Dota2. As of 2022, Danneman somehow got involved in, and inspired to be one of the driving forces behind QHLAN2022, as he is a mastermind planner and loves a good event. He's a technical guy, good with cables and network.
