Markus "myz" Sipola is a Finnish Quakeworld player, coming from the darkest forests of [https://goo.gl/maps/xDMjyVjhVMcPo7b88 Lapland]. Propably most known for his movement skills in 1on1 and 2on2, he was never much of a 4on4 player, although he says he's looking forward to it. He found Quake in 1998 while visiting a friends place and saw his dad, {{Player|Shotqun|flag=fi}} of clan PMC, playing the game. The friends tirelessly waited for the father to fall asleep at night to be able to play it themselves.
