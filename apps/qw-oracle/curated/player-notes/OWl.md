A huge demo-freak, owl started out downloading Fragfest demos in 1997 and got hooked. IRL friends with the founders of The Pooh Clan and got his nick from the clan rule about each having a character from the book. Later got an invite from ruiner who played with Icon of Pain to join them for a gaming session. Later joined them and played from Nine. Was part of the Icon of Pain team that played warmups with Death Row before their games against Clan 9. Moved on to play Quake2 CTF with fuse under the swedish version of the nick owl, "Ugglan". * Real name: Pontus Mårtenson * Born: February 6:th 1978 * Nationality: Swedish * Current clan: -
