In the early days (1996-1999), Greco played in clan TTT alongside Fnys and others, by that time under the nick "BajZ". In 1998 TTT reached 3rd place in the League(?) of 156 playing clans. Later on Fnys moved to SC with Lakerman, Salmiak, Myst, amongst others. Greco attended many LAN tournaments at Cafe9 with (and against) players from Clan 9, clan Icon of Pain and Da Swedish Mob and many, many more. He also participated at the time {{player|Death Row|flag=us}} was invited to Cafe 9, back in '98, and was able to play vs players like Thresh, Da5id, Reptile etc. After 2001, he went more or less into only sporadically playing QW. Grecos favourite map in duel is dm6 and he's considered "very" skilled on that map.
