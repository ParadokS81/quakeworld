Probably one of the most thanked QuakeWorld players. Anyone trying to find anything QW related (movies, sites, leagues) that didn't give out a "Special Thanks To" 1-2-3 (or 1{{$.}}2{{$.}}3 in-game) would have serious problems. But 1-2-3 has meant more to QuakeWorld than just his help behind the scenes. He was one of the founding members of the German clan Imperial Bounty Hunterz, with whom he won numerous German tournaments. When the Imperial Bounty Hunterz went inactive, 1-2-3 temporarily disappeared from competitive teamplay as well. But he didn't last long. The urge to play again became too great when he was asked to join Firing Squad. And despite his long inactivity and some connection problems, he helped Firing Squad win the NQR Invitational Classic Cup and beat their all-time rivals Slackers by playing some great games and making the decisive 2 frags in that cup's final.

## Trivia

* 1-2-3's colors are based on his favorite football team, Mainz 05.
