Kamil also known as rotker is an oldschool Polish QuakeWorld player. He was interested in computers from an early age, and as soon as Quake came out, he started playing it on LAN with his buddies. At that time, they built and maintained a housing estate network from their own resources. Together with friends from the Psychohaloons Crew, he organized several PHC Lan Parties in Wrocław. He played actively mainly TDM until 2010. He mastered the endif map to a master level. After 11 years of break, he returned to the world of QuakeWorld to continue shooting folk with the old friends.
