Dariusz 'Garsut' Obrzut, from Legnica, Wroclaw, is a Polish Quakeworld player of the first era, being a prominent figure in the Polish powerhouse {{Team|Hard Guns Crew|flag=pl}} where he fought alongside his brother {{Player|Badhunter|flag=pl}}. In 2003 he left the scene for studies at the [http://pwr.edu.pl/en/ Wrocław University of Technology], only to resurface in 2021 where he can be spotted warming up with FFA before some fun 2on2 games with his friends.
