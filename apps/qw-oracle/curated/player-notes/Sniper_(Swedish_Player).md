Anders "SnipEr" Lindbäck was a top tier Swedish Quakeworld player during the first era, active between the years 1996 - 1999. In Fragfest '96, he was the second of the {{Flag/se}} Crusaders to play and did a good job, defeating {{Flag/se}} King (KI.) King led out of E1M1 but suffered badly on DM6, losing his advantage and quite a bit more.
