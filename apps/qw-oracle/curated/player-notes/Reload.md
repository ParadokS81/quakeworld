## Information

<b>Favorite mod: 1on1</b><br>
<b>Favorite 1on1 maps: Dm2</b>
<br><br>

'''Reload''' is Australia's most famous QW player ever.<br>
<br>
Participated in TGI in Gothenburg April 1999, 
the first QuakeWorld tournament to gather the best duel players in the world.<br>
Was sponsored by Intel.
