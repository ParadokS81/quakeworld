"FortyLions" is an American Quake Champions commentator. He grew up playing Counter Strike 1.6 and later Global Offensive. In 2018, his caster career began by doing the Quake Champions Worldwide Cup [https://www.plusforward.net/quake/post/64069/FortyLions-Den-Worldwide-Cup-1/ FortyLions’ Dens]. His first official gig was at QuakeCon 2018 Community Cast for Day 1 covering the Open Qualifiers of Quake Champions.

## Trivia

The name 'FortyLions' was born from misreading "''Frothy Loins''" during a chat with his best bud Dare.
