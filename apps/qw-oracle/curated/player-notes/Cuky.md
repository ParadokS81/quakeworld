Lukáš "Cuky" Wiener is a Czech QuakeWorld who has been playing Quake since the 90's. His nickname cuky in Czech means "twitches" and it refers to his unique aim pattern - almost every shot is preceded or followed by a small twitch. Watching his POV for extended periods of time might lead to dizziness. His favourite powerup is Beer Damage.
