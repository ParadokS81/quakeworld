Scott used to play Quakeworld in New Zealand in late 90's and early 00's - mostly under the nickname xus or ninjax (which are now too awkward to say in voice). He lanned with the Dust2Dust (D2D) clan at the LANit venue in Auckland after their triumphant visit to Australia where he focused mostly on team games - CTF, TDM and 2on2 - while only dueling due to boredom. Scott has returned to Quakeworld after almost 20 years and now residing in Australia, again with a keen interest in all things Quakeworld. Category:Players Category:Australian Players __NOTOC__
