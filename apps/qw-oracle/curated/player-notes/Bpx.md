Started playing young in 1999, inspired by his older siblings Atila "Tito" and Laszlo "Lalo" (founder of well-recognized Brazilian teams Savage Hunters and Try Again). bpx continued playing until 2005, though focusing mostly in QWTF. He later engaged in multiple e-sports until 2013, both in playing and management, and came back in 2023 to inject the Quake scene with new ideas and projects. After founding the BPX Foundation in 2023, he promoted tournaments in Quake Champions and in 2024 founded the QW team Psycose which is planned to expand to other games in the near future. Based both in Germany and Brazil, he currently doesn't act as a very active player but is the main manager of Psycose and promote initiatives in e-sports throughout the year, including Quake.
