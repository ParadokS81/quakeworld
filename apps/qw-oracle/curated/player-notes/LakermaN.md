Former QuakeWorld and Q3 superstar LakermaN became an online professional poker player in spring 2004. People say that he won $100.000 in the first 12 months he played poker and his winnings are now estimated over $1.000.000. In his QW days he was considered by some to be the finest all-round player, always retaining his _of_SC suffix. A demo of him ravaging Reaper bots on DM4 was distributed far and wide, ensuring his reputation always preceeded him. [http://web.archive.org/web/20031126063044/http://www.challenge-au.com/events/tgi/page17.html See also this interview with LakermaN] after his TGI win.
