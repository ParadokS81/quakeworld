Robert "Sniket" Edman was a first-generation Quake professional, active from release up until 2000. He was the man behind the deal with Café Nine, which resulted in a sponsorship for his clan Up Your Backpack who then renamed to Clan 9. In 2022 and 2024, he organized QHLAN2022 and QHLAN2024 respectively, in his own event studio, Epic Studios, in Stockholm.
