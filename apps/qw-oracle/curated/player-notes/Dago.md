Jan Dago Schulz is a German old school duel player, especially strong on TB3. Always up for a few duels on dm4 (or dm2 or dm6), his game is improving by the day. After a 16 year long break with studying and work, he decided to return to limelight and become an active player again. His goal, as he puts it, is to find out how people can get more than 40% LG with moving targets.
