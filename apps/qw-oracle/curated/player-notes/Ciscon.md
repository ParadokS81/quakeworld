Bob "ciscon" Nelson is a North American meat popsicle, who has also been attempting to play QuakeWorld properly for the better part of 20 years. While he has not consistently played over the past few decades, he has always run servers in one form or another (rapedangel.net, currently nicotinelounge.com). While his development skills are first to none, he does try to help out where he can in the Quake community.
