Möklu is a famous Finnish Quake/QW player who was especially popular during the years 1999,2000 and 2001. He is known in both Europe and Asia, where he has lived for several years. Möklu's aim is considered to be very fast and accurate, making him a challenging opponent in 1v1.

## Information

* '''Trivia:'''

Möklu's sensitivity is 20cm/360.

Möklu has some unusual habits such as playing with his keyboard on his lap and wearing gloves while playing.

Möklu is from the area called Lapland in the north of Finland.

Möklu is 6'3" tall.

* '''Other games:'''

Möklu has also tried his hand at games such as Unreal Tournament and Counter-Strike.
