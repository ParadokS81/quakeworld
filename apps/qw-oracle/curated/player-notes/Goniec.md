Goniec started playing Quake offline in 2001 mostly playing with Tom, Hubo,NICK and Toniu on LAN. After many local LANs played mainly in internet cafes and Tom's house he moved to Poznan and had good internet connection for the first time. He started to play 4on4 in his first clan The Pimps and then in Boomstick Ownators. He has participated in many Polish LAN Parties and attended QHLAN twice. In 2009 he joined Demolition Crew to play a lot of 4on4s. In 2012 started the longest QuakeWorld break due to family and work issues. Back in 2019 and played for fun with the old crew. Organizer of QW LAN Party Poland 2021.
