Martin "dryg" Andersson is an old-school Quakeworld player who first started playing in 1997. He mostly played CTF. dryg started playing in the CTF clan U:Clan where U:Know taught him how to play with the mouse. Inverted mouse has always been the way he played his FPS games throughout the years. He played with NEC, Hate (H8), and Hellraisers. His last games were played in 2004 when he transitioned to Counterstrike. dryg started playing again in April 2024 after more than 20 years and is one of the team captains for the CTF Showdown 3.
