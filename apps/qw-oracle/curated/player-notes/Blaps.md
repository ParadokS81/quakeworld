Oldie quaker who started playing RQ when the leaked Quake for MacOS surfaced. Played RQ mostly on TheZoo ffa-server located at Stockholm Uni where he also studied. The european RQ-scene came to an abrupt end when TheZoo, as one of the last RQ-servers in existance, had to shut down. Later switched to QW and still plays occasionally. Btw, the name blaps comes from the scientific name for a rare swedish beetle (blaps mortisaga) who's swedish trivial name is Dödsbud, which in turn translates to "messenger of death". Very cool ;).
