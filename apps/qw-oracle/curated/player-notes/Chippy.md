## Information

Trivia:
Started playing with demo version back in '96 when it was on a cover CD.  Played it online and got me hooked on the internet and spent silly amounts on the phone bills (well my mum's money!) ;P
Got me hooked on the internet and it takes me back to the days of 200-300 ping was quite playable and the odd modem cable sign appearing was normal.  On great servers like wildnet and minos when they were filled with crazyed keyboard a few mouse-wielding LPB's.
Joined and played with the wonderful CTF clan The Buddha finger - HanDoJin Paz Splitter (many more when I can remember!).  The grappling hook was my favorite weapon!
Joined O$ and if I find any timedemo's I'll tell you who they were! It was THAT long ago.
Joined [MoD] to play Clan Arena, my 2nd love of Quake.  Greets to the wonderful team - Reverend & Creamylust<BR>
Joined ]MB[ afterwards with what was left, which was great, Tronus organized a few wonderful clan meetings and that was really the last time I've properly played.  Around 2005 I think.  But still want to meet up so don't hesitate to shot me an email :)
