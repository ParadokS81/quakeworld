Jon "jOn" Rinneby is a Swedish Quakeworld player who started his career in 1999 after moving from the city of Lund to small town Tranås (nothing to do there except eating hot dogs and playing QW). With staggering steps and high ping his career began under the name RpBot, later changed to jOn sometimes in early 2000.
