* Real name: Mikael Oinonen * Born: 1983 * Nationality: Finnish * Current clan: In a Blaze

## Trivia

First person ever to win Clanbase Nations Cup for two different nations (Team Sweden and Team Finland 2002 and 2003).<br>
One of the founding members of www.callofduty.fi and www.dayofdefeat.fi -community websites. <br>
Community work in Clanbase Crew during the years 2002-2006, including cup supervision for various Call of Duty Eurocups, Opencups and Nations Cups. <br>
Mod-supervisor for Call of Duty:United Offensive 2004-2005.<br>
Played in the first Call of Duty 1 -official Clanbase game in 8th of November 2003.<br>
Manager of Team Finlands national teams under Clanbase. <br>
Admin of QWTF SM-League 1999 and 2000. <br>
Head admin of Scandinavian Open QWTF League 2001.<br>
Head admin of Scandinavian DoD League 2002. <br>
