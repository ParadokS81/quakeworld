 Cream is an American player (from America) who began playing and dominating QuakeWorld TDM in the middle of 2011. Nobody knows what caused him to decide to play QW, but everyone is grateful that he does. His arrival in the NA QW scene was a pleasant surprise to everyone, including himself. Cream's refreshing sense of style has brought many changes to the way QW is played in the USA. His pro QW skills have been sharpened and refined like a knife, which is why everyone is afraid of him.
