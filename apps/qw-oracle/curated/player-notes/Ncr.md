ncr is a player from the south of Sweden. <br /> He started playing Quake in 1997 and QuakeWorld in 1998. <br /> Member of the clan Allans Vittnen, together with xtr, they initiated the clan's comeback as an active 4on4 clan in 2006 after a hiatus of six years. <br /> After 12 years of inactivity, ncr came back to QuakeWorld again around Christmas 2020. <br /> He has been an enthused participant of competitions in 1on1, 2on2 and 4on4 since.
