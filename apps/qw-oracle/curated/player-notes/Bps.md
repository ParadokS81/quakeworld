__NOTOC__ Jon "bps" Cednert is a Swedish QuakeWorld player with a long and active career under his belt. After having played {{Abbr|RQ|Real Quake/Net Quake/Vanilla Quake}} silently up until 2001, he slowly earned reputation as a relentless leader of clan {{player|Suddendeath|flag=se}}, grinding his way to the top in 2009.
