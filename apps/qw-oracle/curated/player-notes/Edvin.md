Edvin began playing QuakeWorld briefly back in 1999 at the age of 8, after looking over the shoulders of his older brothers Mythic & Mille playing the game. After quitting QW around 2009, Edvin returned to QuakeWorld just days before New Year’s Eve 2025. He was persuaded by his brothers and real-life friend Sailorman, whom Edvin originally introduced to QuakeWorld back in the days, to start playing again for ving.
