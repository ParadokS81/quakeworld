Tom "Entropy" Kimzey was one of the lead rivals to the supremacy of Dennis Fong (Thresh) in the Quake competition. Similarly to Fong, Kimzey was a student at the University of Kansas. Kimzey had made it to the grand final of the 1997 Red Annihilation Tournament, however was dominated by Fong in a 14-1 defeat. With Quake opening the potential for team based play, Kimzey was better known for being one of the original pioneers of a gaming team (clan). Kimzey represented his team, Impulse 9 in the Red Annihilation Tournament. Taken from [https://esports.fandom.com/ esport.fandom]
