Nilton "CrAsH" de Souza is a Brazilian Quakeworld and fomer Netquake player who currently plays CTF and FFA from Prague. He started in 1996 playing Deathmatch and CTF (netquake) in Brazil and stopped in 1999. In 1997, together with his Brazilian friends, he created the clan called "BMW" - Brazilian Mercenary Wolves. After 21 years he is back, with a late first contact with Quakeworld. He mostly plays FFA at the servegame server and occasionally joins small tournaments organised by {{Player|bgnr|flag=pt}}, while always ready for any upcoming CTF action.
