BORYS is a Polish Doom/Doom II and Quake/Quake II map maker. He worked with WorldCraft tool to create such Quake maps as BlueYard, CChain1, Metalbox, TheLamp, Oldpalace, Moat, Abrhplus, Windlift, Froqe, Rzaba2, Outpost and Fragteh1.

## Trivia

Currently works as an architect.
