brief introduction Began playing together with blaps on his old mac back in 1996. Remember we got hold of a leaked alpha of quake for mac. Later on we started both the clan ZOO as well as the ran the ZOO server with the infamous Dancehall that saw its timely demise in late 2002. After that did I only intermittently have any Internet connection or machine to play on. I did a brief come back with SM during a Quake LAN in early 2003, however. But it was not until mid 2004 that I managed to build myself a new computer (then in the US) and later the same year I started my career in HL2DM where you will currently (2008) find me. Since early 2007 I'm back on the European scene though.
