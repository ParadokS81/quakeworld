## Summary

1on1 is QuakeWorld's premier solo format: two players, no teams, fighting to out-frag each other over a 10-minute match. Because it runs `deathmatch 3` -- weapons stay on the floor after they are picked up -- the duel is not about denying your opponent weapons; both players can always reach the rocket launcher. The contest is aim and item *timing*: controlling the red armor, the mega health and the weapon spawns so that you meet every fight with more stack than your opponent. It is the most-played individual discipline in the game, fought on a small, stable set of classic duel maps.

## How it plays

Two players spawn into the same map and frag each other for ten minutes; the higher frag count wins, and a draw goes to a 3-minute overtime. There are no teams (`teamplay 0`) and the duel discriminator `k_mode 1` is set, so the whole match is one player against one.

What shapes the play is the deathmatch flag. From two players or fewer KTX runs `deathmatch 3`: a weapon you pick up stays where it lay, so the rocket launcher and lightning gun are always available to both players. (The larger formats -- 3on3 and up -- switch to `deathmatch 1`, where picked-up weapons vanish and weapon control becomes the game. That line falls between 2on2 and 3on3.) With weapons effectively free, a duel is decided by aim and by the timing battle over the items that *do* respawn on a clock: red and yellow armor, mega health, and ammo. The skill is knowing those timers, contesting them, and forcing your opponent to fight you understacked. Unlike the team modes, the standard 1on1 preset leaves powerups off (`k_pow 0`), so the default duel is a pure armor-mega-and-aim game rather than a quad fight.

## Starting a game

On a KTX server, type `/1on1` in the console. It sets up a match rather than interrupting one, so it is a pre-match action; both players join (the mode caps at two) and ready up. You can append a match tag for demo and QTV naming -- `1on1 EQL`. The server has to allow the mode -- if `k_allowed_free_modes` does not include the 1on1 bit the command is refused -- and it is blocked on hoonymode-only maps; see Hosting & settings.

## Maps

1on1 is played on a small, stable rotation of maps built or chosen for duelling, not the full id1 set. The classics are `dm2`, `dm4`, and `dm6`, alongside the community staples `aerowalk` and `ztndm3`. These are scene conventions rather than anything KTX enforces -- any map will load, but organized duelling sticks to the tested set, where item layouts and timings are deeply understood by the players.

## History

1on1 predates KTX, carried forward from the KTPro era into KTX as a built-in usermode the same way the team formats were. It is the deepest individual discipline in QuakeWorld, with a long international competitive scene -- the top duellists have come from across the Nordic countries, the Netherlands, the UK and beyond -- and the duel has always been the format in which individual skill is measured most directly.

## Hosting & settings

1on1 rides on the `UM_1ON1` bit, value `1` -- its own bit, shared with no other mode. On a stock KTX or nquake server `k_allowed_free_modes` defaults to `4095` (every standard mode), so 1on1 is available out of the box; you set the mask explicitly only to *restrict* a server to a subset.

```
# server.cfg -- 4095 is the stock default and already allows 1on1 (the 1 bit)
set k_allowed_free_modes 4095
```

The 1on1 ruleset is fixed in KTX and applied by the `/1on1` command. The settings that define it:

- **`deathmatch 3`** -- weapons stay on pickup; the reason a duel is an aim-and-timing game rather than a weapon-control one.
- **`k_mode 1` / `teamplay 0`** -- the duel discriminator, no teams.
- **`maxclients 2`** -- two players.
- **`timelimit 10`** with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 3`** -- 10-minute matches and a 3-minute overtime on a draw (shorter than the team modes' 5).
- **`k_pow 0`** -- powerups off by default, unlike the team modes (`k_pow 1`). A surprising value worth knowing: the stock duel has no quad.
