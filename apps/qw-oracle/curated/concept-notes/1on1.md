## Summary

1on1 is QuakeWorld's premier solo format: two players, no teams, out-fragging each other over a 10-minute match. Weapons stay on the floor when picked up, so the duel is not about denying your opponent a rocket launcher -- it comes down to aim and to *timing*: controlling the red armor, the mega health and the weapon spawns so you meet every fight with the bigger stack. It is the deepest individual discipline in QuakeWorld, fought on a small, stable set of classic duel maps. You start a game with `/1on1`.

## Activate

On a KTX server, type `/1on1` in the console. Both players type `ready` to begin the countdown and match. You can append a match tag for demo and QTV naming -- `/1on1 DuelmaniaFinals`.

## Basic ruleset

Activating 1on1 applies a fixed duel preset:

- **`deathmatch 3`** -- weapons stay on the floor after pickup, so both players can always reach the key weapons (the reason a duel is an aim-and-timing game; see *How it plays*).
- **`k_mode 1` / `teamplay 0`** -- the duel discriminator; no teams, and you still take your own splash damage.
- **`maxclients 2`** -- two players.
- **`timelimit 10`**, with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 3`** -- 10-minute matches; a draw goes to a single 3-minute timed overtime.
- **`k_pow 0`** -- powerups off; the stock duel spawns no Quad, Pent or Ring.

## How it plays

Two players spawn into the same map and frag each other for ten minutes; the higher frag count wins, and a draw goes to a 3-minute overtime. There are no teams (`teamplay 0`) and the duel discriminator `k_mode 1` is set, so the whole match is one player against one.

What shapes the play is the deathmatch flag. 1on1 mode runs `deathmatch 3`: A weapon you pick up stays on the map. With weapons effectively free, a duel is decided by aim and by the timing battle over the items that *do* respawn on a clock: red and yellow armor (20 seconds after pickup), mega health (20 seconds after expiration), and ammo (15 seconds after pickup). The skill is knowing those timers, contesting them, and forcing your opponent to fight you understacked. Unlike the team modes, the standard 1on1 preset leaves powerups off (`k_pow 0`), so the default duel is a pure armor-mega-and-aim game rather than a quad fight.

## Maps

1on1 is played on a small, stable rotation built or chosen for duelling, not the full id1 set -- scene convention, not anything KTX enforces (any map loads, but organized duelling sticks to the tested set, where the item layouts and timings are deeply understood).

- **Classics:** `dm2`, `dm4`, `dm6`, plus the community staples `aerowalk` and `ztndm3`.
- **Modern competitive (maphub_v2):** `catalyst`, `bravado`, `pocket`, `spitfire`, `toxicity`, and winter `frost` variants of the classics.

## History

1on1 predates KTX, carried forward from the KTPro era into KTX as a built-in usermode the same way the team formats were. The duel is where individual skill is measured most directly, and it has the deepest competitive history of any QuakeWorld format -- recurring online series like Duelmania and the QuakeWorld Duel League, the annual QHLAN LAN, and a long line of famous duellists from across the Nordic countries, the Netherlands, the UK and beyond.

## Hosting & settings

On a stock KTX or nquake server 1on1 is available by default -- the allow-list (`k_allowed_free_modes`) defaults to `4095`, every mode. For a duel-only server:

```
set k_defmode 1on1            // boot into duel
set k_allowed_free_modes 1    // allow nothing else  (default 4095 = all modes)
```

See *server-setup* for the bitmask details.

To run the duel *with* powerups (the preset ships them off), set `k_pow 1` in `configs/usermodes/1on1/default.cfg` -- the per-usermode config execs after the preset, so a `server.cfg` setting would be overwritten. That file is also where you layer any other duel-server overrides.
