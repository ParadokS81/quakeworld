## Summary

2on2 is two-on-two team deathmatch, the smallest of the team formats. Weapons stay on the floor, so it is a fast, aim-driven game where the rocket launcher is always available; the contest is coordinated control of the armour, mega health and powerups between two-player teams, with friendly fire on. The higher team frag count at the 10-minute mark wins. You start a game with `/2on2`.

## Activate

On a KTX server, type `/2on2` in the console. Each team readies up (`ready`) to begin the countdown and match. You can append a match tag for demo and QTV naming -- `/2on2 EQL`.

## Basic ruleset

Activating 2on2 applies a fixed preset:

- **`deathmatch 3`** -- weapons stay on the floor after pickup; the rocket launcher and lightning gun are always available.
- **`teamplay 2`** -- friendly fire on (your splash damages your partner).
- **`k_mode 2` / `maxclients 4`** -- two teams of two.
- **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live: Quad, Pentagram and Ring spawn on the map.
- **`timelimit 10`**, with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 3`** -- 10-minute matches; a draw goes to a 3-minute timed overtime.
- **`k_membercount 1`** -- one ready player per team to start.

## How it plays

Two teams of two fight to out-frag each other; the higher team count at ten minutes wins, and a draw goes to a three-minute overtime. Friendly fire is on, so your own splash damages your partner -- spamming a corridor your teammate is contesting can cost you a frag.

Weapons stay on the floor, so neither team is ever denied firepower; both can always arm up, and the game turns on aim and on control of the items that respawn on a timer -- the red armour and mega health, and the powerups. With only two players a side, a Quad is a short, decisive window that a coordinated pair can turn into a multi-frag swing, so calling and timing the powerups matters as much as the shooting. The rest of the game is what two players do together: trading map control, covering each other's item runs, and doubling up on a caught-out opponent.

## Maps

2on2 is played on a small, stable rotation of compact maps whose item layouts suit a two-player-per-side fight -- scene convention, not anything KTX enforces.

- **Classics:** `dm2`, `dm4`, `dm6`, `aerowalk`, `ztndm3`.
- **Modern competitive (maphub_v2):** `catalyst`, `bravado`, and the broader curated pool.

## Hosting & settings

On a stock KTX or nquake server 2on2 is available by default -- the allow-list (`k_allowed_free_modes`) defaults to `4095`, every mode. For a 2on2-only server:

```
set k_defmode 2on2            // boot into 2on2
set k_allowed_free_modes 2    // allow nothing else  (default 4095 = all modes)
```

See *server-setup* for the bitmask details.
