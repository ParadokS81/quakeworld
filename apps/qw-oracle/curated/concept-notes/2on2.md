## Summary

2on2 is two-on-two team deathmatch, the smallest of the team formats. It sits right next to the duel: like 1on1 it runs `deathmatch 3`, so weapons stay on the floor and the game is aim plus the timing of armor, mega health and ammo rather than weapon denial. What 2on2 adds is a teammate -- friendly fire is on, you coordinate item control and pushes between two players, and unlike the bare duel the powerups are live, so quad timing is back on the table. The higher team frag count at the 10-minute mark wins.

## How it plays

Two teams of two, friendly fire on (`teamplay 2`). Because it runs `deathmatch 3` -- weapons stay on the ground after pickup -- 2on2 inherits the duel's economy rather than 4on4's: the rocket launcher is always available, so neither team can starve the other of weapons, and the contest is aim plus control of the items that respawn on a timer. (The weapons-stay line falls exactly here: 2on2 is the last `deathmatch 3` format; from 3on3 up KTX switches to `deathmatch 1` and weapon control becomes the game.)

The step up from the duel is coordination. With a teammate you trade map control, cover each other's item runs, and double up on a caught-out opponent -- but friendly splash counts, so spamming a contested corridor can cost your own partner. And unlike the standard 1on1 preset, 2on2 has powerups live (`k_pow 1`): a quad in a 2v2 is a short, decisive window that a coordinated pair can convert into a multi-frag swing. A draw goes to a 3-minute overtime.

## Starting a game

On a KTX server, type `/2on2` in the console -- a pre-match action that sets the mode up rather than interrupting a running match. Each team needs at least one ready player before the match starts (`k_membercount 1`); then both teams ready up. You can append a match tag for demo and QTV naming -- `2on2 EQL`. The server has to allow the mode (see Hosting & settings).

## Maps

2on2 is played on the same small-map rotation as the duel -- the classics `dm2`, `dm4`, `dm6`, `aerowalk`, and `ztndm3`, plus the broader curated duel pool (`catalyst`, `bravado`, and the rest of the `maphub_v2` 1on1 set) -- the maps whose item layouts and timings suit a one- or two-player-per-side fight. As with the other formats this is scene convention, not anything KTX enforces.

## Hosting & settings

2on2 rides on the `UM_2ON2` bit, value `2` -- its own bit, shared with no other mode. On a stock KTX or nquake server `k_allowed_free_modes` defaults to `4095`, so 2on2 is available out of the box; set the mask explicitly only to *restrict* the server to a subset.

```
# server.cfg -- 4095 is the stock default and already allows 2on2 (the 2 bit)
set k_allowed_free_modes 4095
```

The 2on2 ruleset is fixed in KTX and applied by `/2on2`. The settings that define it:

- **`deathmatch 3`** -- weapons stay on pickup; the same economy as the duel.
- **`k_mode 2` / `teamplay 2`** -- team mode, friendly fire on.
- **`maxclients 4`** -- two teams of two.
- **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live (the duel turns them off; every team format leaves them on).
- **`timelimit 10`** with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 3`** -- 10-minute matches, 3-minute overtime on a draw.
- **`k_membercount 1`** -- one ready player per team minimum.
