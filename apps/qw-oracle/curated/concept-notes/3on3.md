## Summary

3on3 is three-on-three team deathmatch, and it sits on the important side of the weapons line: from three players a side, KTX runs `deathmatch 1`, so a weapon you pick up does not respawn where it lay. That turns the rocket launcher, lightning gun, and the map's armor and health into finite, timed resources a team can control and deny -- the same economy that defines 4on4, just at a smaller roster. Matches run 15 minutes and the higher team frag count wins.

## How it plays

Two teams of three, friendly fire on (`teamplay 2`). The defining change from the small formats is the deathmatch flag: 3on3 is the first size to run `deathmatch 1`, where picked-up weapons vanish instead of staying on the floor. (1on1 and 2on2 sit just under that line on `deathmatch 3`.) So 3on3 is a map-control game -- the team that times the red armor and the key weapons, and denies them to the other side, builds the advantage -- rather than the pure aim-and-stack duel that the smaller formats are. Powerups are live (`k_pow 1`), so quad control matters as it does in 4on4. It plays like a lighter 4on4: the same priorities of item timing and coordinated positioning, with one fewer player a side and a 15-minute clock (a draw goes to a 5-minute overtime).

## Starting a game

On a KTX server, type `/3on3` in the console -- a pre-match action. Each team needs two ready players before the match starts (`k_membercount 2`), then both teams ready up. A match tag can be appended for demo and QTV naming (`3on3 EQL`). The server has to allow the mode (see Hosting & settings).

## Maps

3on3 draws from the same competitive team-map pool as 4on4, favoring the smaller maps in it -- `dm3` above all, along with `dm2` and `e1m2`. As with the other formats the rotation is scene convention rather than anything KTX enforces.

## Hosting & settings

3on3 rides on the `UM_3ON3` bit, value `4` -- its own bit. On a stock KTX or nquake server `k_allowed_free_modes` defaults to `4095`, so 3on3 is available out of the box; set the mask explicitly only to *restrict* the server.

```
# server.cfg -- 4095 is the stock default and already allows 3on3 (the 4 bit)
set k_allowed_free_modes 4095
```

The defining settings, applied by `/3on3`:

- **`deathmatch 1`** -- weapons disappear on pickup; the reason 3on3 is a map-control game, and the line that separates it from 2on2.
- **`k_mode 2` / `teamplay 2`** -- team mode, friendly fire on.
- **`maxclients 6`** -- two teams of three.
- **`timelimit 15`** with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 5`** -- 15-minute matches, 5-minute overtime on a draw.
- **`k_membercount 2`** -- two ready players per team minimum. **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live.
