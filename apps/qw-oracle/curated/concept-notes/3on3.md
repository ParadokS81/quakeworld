## Summary

3on3 is three-on-three team deathmatch -- the smallest roster where picked-up weapons vanish and respawn rather than staying on the floor, which turns the map's armour, powerups and weapon spawns into contested, timed resources. So it is a control-and-timing team game, not a pure fragfest: two teams of three play to the higher frag count over a 15-minute match. You start a game with `/3on3`.

## Activate

On a KTX server, type `/3on3` in the console; both teams ready up (`ready`) to begin. You can append a match tag for demo and QTV naming -- `/3on3 EQL`.

## Basic ruleset

Activating 3on3 applies a fixed preset:

- **`deathmatch 1`** -- a picked-up weapon vanishes and respawns 30 seconds later instead of staying on the floor, so weapons join the armour and powerups as timed, contested resources.
- **`teamplay 2`** -- friendly fire on.
- **`k_mode 2` / `maxclients 6`** -- two teams of three.
- **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live.
- **`timelimit 15`**, with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 5`** -- 15-minute matches; a draw goes to a 5-minute timed overtime.
- **`k_membercount 2`** -- two ready players per team to start.

## How it plays

Two teams of three. Because weapons vanish when picked up and only respawn on a timer, you can't count on instantly rearming -- so the map's resources (the red armour above all, the powerups, the weapon spawns) become the contested ground. The game is controlling those items and their timings, holding map areas, and converting the advantage in 3v3 fights, run on constant team reporting. At three a side it is the smallest roster that plays this controlled team game.

## Maps

3on3 never really caught on as its own format -- when it runs, it plays on the smaller team maps (`dm3`, `dm2`) rather than a dedicated pool.

## Hosting & settings

On a stock KTX or nquake server 3on3 is available by default -- the allow-list (`k_allowed_free_modes`) defaults to `4095`, every mode. For a 3on3-only server:

```
set k_defmode 3on3            // boot into 3on3
set k_allowed_free_modes 4    // allow nothing else  (default 4095 = all modes)
```

See *server-setup* for the bitmask details.
