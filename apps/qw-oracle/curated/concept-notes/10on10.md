## Summary

10on10 is big-team deathmatch: ten players a side, twenty on the server at once. Mechanically it is 4on4 scaled up -- `deathmatch 1`, so weapons vanish on pickup and item control is the game -- but at that many players the experience is its own thing: a loud, crowded fight where the map is never quiet and individual item timing gives way to sheer team pressure across a large arena. It is the biggest of the standard-game formats and needs maps built to hold the crowd.

## How it plays

Two teams of ten, friendly fire on (`teamplay 2`), on `deathmatch 1` -- the same weapon-control economy as 4on4, but the scale changes how it feels. With twenty players in a level, the fights are constant and overlapping, splash is everywhere, and no single player controls a timing the way a 4on4 player owns the red armor. It becomes a game of team waves and map pressure more than precise item rotations. Powerups are live (`k_pow 1`), matches run 20 minutes with a 5-minute overtime, and each team needs five ready players before the match starts (`k_membercount 5`).

## Starting a game

On a KTX server, type `/10on10` in the console -- a pre-match action. It only makes sense on a large map with enough players to fill it; the preset caps the server at twenty (`maxclients 20`). A match tag can be appended for demo and QTV naming. The server has to allow the mode (see Hosting & settings).

## Maps

10on10 needs maps built for big-team play -- the standard 4on4 and duel maps are far too small for twenty players. The community-curated large-map set (from the `maphub_v2` server hub) headlines with **`death32c`**, the definitive big-team map, built to hold even larger games than 10on10's twenty -- it is the map of the legendary nation-versus-nation matches of decades past, which ran as large as 16-on-16. Alongside it sit **`superdm32`**, **`bloodwalk`** (a megamap that stitches together two `ztndm3` arenas and an `aerowalk`), and **`schobble`**. These are scene conventions; KTX enforces only the player cap, not the map.

## Hosting & settings

10on10 rides on the `UM_10ON10` bit, value `16` -- its own bit. On a stock KTX or nquake server `k_allowed_free_modes` defaults to `4095`, so 10on10 is available out of the box; set the mask explicitly only to *restrict* the server.

```
# server.cfg -- 4095 is the stock default and already allows 10on10 (the 16 bit)
set k_allowed_free_modes 4095
```

The defining settings, applied by `/10on10`:

- **`maxclients 20`** -- ten players a side. The largest cap of the fixed-roster standard modes (XonX goes higher with a variable roster).
- **`deathmatch 1`** -- weapons vanish on pickup, the 4on4 economy.
- **`k_mode 2` / `teamplay 2`** -- team mode, friendly fire on.
- **`timelimit 20`** with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 5`** -- 20-minute matches, 5-minute overtime.
- **`k_membercount 5`** -- five ready players per team minimum (the highest of the family). **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live.
