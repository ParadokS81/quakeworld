## Summary

10on10 is big-team deathmatch -- ten players a side, twenty on the server. It runs the weapon-control economy at scale on a small set of purpose-built large maps: with twenty players in one level the fights are constant and overlapping, so it is a game of team waves and map pressure more than precise item rotations. It is the biggest fixed-roster standard format. You start a game with `/10on10`.

## Activate

On a KTX server, type `/10on10` in the console; it only makes sense on a large map with enough players to fill it. Both teams ready up (`ready`) to begin. A match tag can be appended for demo and QTV naming.

## Basic ruleset

Activating 10on10 applies a fixed preset:

- **`deathmatch 1`** -- weapons vanish on pickup and respawn on a timer; the weapon-control economy.
- **`teamplay 2` / `k_mode 2`** -- two teams, friendly fire on.
- **`maxclients 20`** -- ten players a side; the largest cap of the fixed-roster standard modes.
- **`k_pow 1`** -- powerups live.
- **`timelimit 20`**, with **`k_overtime 1` / `k_exttime 5`** -- 20-minute matches; a draw goes to a 5-minute overtime.
- **`k_membercount 5`** -- five ready players per team to start (the highest of the family).

## How it plays

Two teams of ten. With twenty players in a level the fights are constant and overlapping, splash is everywhere, and no single player owns an item timing the way a small-team player owns the red armour. It becomes a game of team waves and map pressure across a large arena rather than precise item rotations -- the weapon-control economy at a scale where numbers and positioning decide it.

## Maps

10on10 needs maps built for big-team play -- the small team and duel maps are far too cramped for twenty players.

- **Big-team pool (maphub_v2):** `death32c` -- the definitive big-team map, built to hold even larger games than 10on10's twenty; it is the map of the legendary nation-vs-nation matches of decades past, which ran as large as 16-on-16. Alongside it: `superdm32`, `bloodwalk` (a megamap stitching two `ztndm3` arenas and an `aerowalk` together), and `schobble`.

## Hosting & settings

On a stock KTX or nquake server 10on10 is available by default -- the allow-list (`k_allowed_free_modes`) defaults to `4095`, every mode. For a 10on10-only server:

```
set k_defmode 10on10           // boot into 10on10
set k_allowed_free_modes 16    // allow nothing else  (default 4095 = all modes)
```

See *server-setup* for the bitmask details.
