**Cross-app contract drift sidequest** (commits `6fbe46c` + `b495e5a`). Operator-driven verification surfaced a JSONB serialization bug in 8 loader sites: `JSON.stringify` on JS arrays/objects before binding to JSONB params caused postgres-js to JSON-encode the string a second time, storing JSONB string scalars. Slipgate's `macros` and `cmdline-params` snapshots were broken at the contract boundary. Fix: drop `JSON.stringify` in `load-{macros,cmdline-params,rulesets,hud-elements,info-keys,log-templates,release-notes,gameplay}`; `natural-keys.ts` wraps affected JSONB bindings with `tx.json(...)` for postgres-js type compliance; `types.ts` updates 9 row-type fields from `string | null` to typed array shapes. DB repaired by re-extracting all 18 (project, version) tuples through the corrected loader; 37 entity_defs + 41 mechanics stale duplicate rows deleted from gameplay tables (unique key included the broken JSONB column, so ON CONFLICT couldn't replace in place). New regression probe `F1.jsonb_columns_not_strings` covers all 13 affected (table, column) pairs and reports PASS. Slipgate `bundle.ts` updated: `source_verified` + `dev_only` from `number` to `boolean`. The 4 ezquake snapshot files (`variables`/`commands`/`macros`/`cmdline-params`) regenerated through the fixed pipeline; 3 of 4 byte-identical to the pre-bug 8ed16de baseline; `variables.json` adds the legitimate `s_stereo` cvar from a fresh extract.
